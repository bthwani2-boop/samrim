# Customer Store and catalog discovery

CAPABILITY_ID: CUSTOMER_STORE_CATALOG
ARTIFACT_CLASS: DERIVED_SPEC_KIT_REQUIREMENTS

Business decisions belong to [Stores](../../governance/stores.md), [Catalog](../../governance/catalog.md), [Commerce](../../governance/commerce.md), [Identity](../../governance/identity.md) and [Experience](../../governance/experience.md). This feature describes observable behavior, not policy authority or acceptance.

- AC-01: DSH returns only eligible published MARKETPLACE Stores in the selected active city/vertical, with bounded deterministic pagination. DIRECT may have its authorized full public Web storefront without marketplace listing. Withdrawn, private and out-of-scope records never leak. Published closed/paused Stores remain visible only as allowed, not orderable.
- AC-02: Store details and Offers use canonical owner projections, eligible Product/Variant/Offer, applicable category, attributes, modifiers, quantity/stock and positive exact YER prices. No draft/private attributes, unapproved media, grants, addresses, credentials or financial details. Invalid identifiers, stale revisions, changed prices and cursor conflicts fail or reconcile safely.
- AC-03: Customer Web/Android displays real owner data, accessible Arabic RTL, light/dark, empty/loading/error/retry, filter, stable links and native back behavior. Changing city/Store/account prevents stale response publication. Disconnection, retry and withdrawn publication never create invented cards or orderability. Test keyboard/focus, font scaling and relevant reduced-motion behavior.
- AC-04: Authenticated favorite Stores use the DSH actor-scoped set and current Identity Customer session. Add only currently published eligible Stores, including lawful DIRECT public links without listing them in marketplace; hide withdrawn Stores, allow removal. Bounded live keyset list (20), no actor selector. GET one state and PUT exact boolean desired state; identical requests converge. Unknown result requires readback before another action. Reject foreign/revoked role, malformed identifiers/query/body, duplicate fields and cross-site writes.
- AC-05: Favorite list and Store-detail toggle use the existing same-origin Web session boundary and verified native transport; prove durable add/remove, isolation, revocation, outage/recovery, visibility and pagination. Offer favorites are separate.
- AC-06: Clearing a nonempty cart requires explicit confirmation bound to the verified Store, account and cart version. Cancellation dispatches nothing. Show empty cart only after the existing DSH versioned replacement confirms an empty line list; lost result retains operation identity for reconciliation. Refresh, actor/scope change, conflict or sign-out invalidates confirmation.
- AC-07: Discovery or saved/cleared cart is not Order creation, payment, stock reservation or confirmation. Future checkout independently revalidates current price, inventory, availability, channel, fulfillment and applicable financial terms.

Acceptance requires actual owner contract and isolated database/HTTP readback, appropriate negative visibility/authorization, cursor, concurrency, interruption/recovery and consumer tests, plus affected Web/Android interaction. Unrun iOS, protected data upgrade and production are separate. No new business owner or duplicate data engine.
