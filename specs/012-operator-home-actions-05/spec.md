# Feature specification — Operator Home actions

Derived DESIGN-01/DESIGN-03 scope under [experience](../../governance/experience.md) and [identity](../../governance/identity.md).

Verified Operators discover authorized work centers from Home, including Marketing, HR, Policies and separately verified original-Operator Access. No new permission, metric or service.

- AC1: Every permitted primary entry except Home appears once in quick actions and the complete permission directory; no arbitrary item cap. Verify single-scope Marketing/HR and all-grants.
- AC2: Use canonical visibleWorkspaceEntries(grants, originalOperator) and current verified actor authority. Revoked/unverified/ordinary Operators never see original-only access. No route/grant changes.
- AC3: Quick actions precede optional owner-backed market data; Arabic RTL, keyboard links, correct icons and responsive width. Counts describe visible destinations, not orders/revenue/balance. No fabricated metrics or access.
- AC4: Actual permission, route/component tests, affected type/lint, Next/browser, authorized owner/runtime and exact CI. No API/SQL/service changes.
