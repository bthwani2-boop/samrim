## 7. Git delivery

Deliver each complete, owned, tested foundation immediately. Standing or specific human Push authority permits only ordinary fast-forward publication to the authorized unprotected development upstream. No global cleanliness prerequisite, peer completion prerequisite, dedicated reservation, coordinator, lock or wrapper. Main/protected branches, archive/**, other remotes, new branches/worktrees/PRs, merges, deployment and release require their own authority; Force Push and history rewriting must not displace peer work.

1. Establish the actual repository, remote, branch, upstream, protections/rulesets, remote SHA, ownership and index. Record the reviewed base and exact owned tested path/blob inventory, applicable tests and secret/diff/UTF-8 checks. Stage and commit only those paths using native Git, preserving unrelated staged and unstaged work. No blanket staging, empty commits, Reset/Rebase/Stash or inclusion of unapproved index entries.
2. Freeze the full immutable commit SHA; verify its parent, exact committed files/blobs and every outgoing ancestor relative to the observed remote. A moving HEAD or branch ref cannot authorize extra content. Reconcile concrete conflicts only; unapproved outgoing ancestors or non-fast-forward block that Push, not independent work.
3. Publish with ordinary fast-forward Git: git push <verified-remote> <verified-full-commit-sha>:refs/heads/<verified-upstream-branch>. Never push symbolic HEAD or a moving local ref. If the candidate, index, remote or authority changed materially, reconcile before publication. Never force.
4. Read back the remote ref and immutable published commit content. Equality proves delivery of that exact candidate. If an authorized successor advanced the ref, establish the published SHA as an ancestor of the actual remote SHA and verify its immutable blobs. Distinguish unchanged relevant content from superseded content: record the actual successor SHA and changed owned paths, invalidate affected current-state evidence and require successor-specific acceptance. Do not claim remote equality or certify the successor from historical delivery. If ancestry, authorization or content cannot be established, delivery readback remains unresolved. Reconcile ambiguous effects before retry; preserve peer changes and index entries.
5. Inspect mandatory CI, security and quality gates for the exact applicable candidate. Publication is delivery, not acceptance. Pending, failed, unavailable or invalidated proof leaves only its affected scope open. Preserve unaffected valid evidence; rerun affected and mandatory direct checks. Continue independent work immediately; do not infer journey, environment, governance or production readiness from publication.
