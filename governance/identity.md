## IDENTITY_ACTIVATION_SESSIONS — Identity activation and authorized sessions
CAPABILITY_ID: IDENTITY_ACTIVATION_SESSIONS

Identity alone owns the stable canonical actor ID, verified contact and official identity name, authentication factors, high-level role admission, sessions, recovery and platform permissions. One human may hold several independently authorized roles from the canonical set in [platform](platform.md); a role is not a resource grant. DSH owns Store, employment, operational standing and Order facts; WLT owns financial balances, instruments and claims. Employment, delegation, standing, beneficiary and delivery recipient remain distinct. No employee-specific identity, UI-derived role or second account for another presentation of the same Customer.

Phone is a mutable verified attribute, never the actor identifier. Official Identity names are distinct from Store display labels, temporary names, onboarding input and beneficiary data. Resolve an eligible verified contact to the existing canonical actor; reject duplicate actors, caller-chosen principals and impersonation by invitation or assisted provisioning. A domain-submitted name is input until accepted by Identity.

## Role admission, enrollment and authentication

Verification of a person, domain eligibility, Identity role admission, factor enrollment, authentication, session issuance and resource authorization are distinct facts. An admitted operation may atomically combine only its expressly authorized transitions. Registration or activation cannot be reused as ordinary login or reactivate disabled roles. A credential or session for one role cannot authenticate another; a specifically admitted role-admission or re-entry operation may consume a current verified source-role proof to issue a distinct target-role credential, never reinterpret the source credential as a target-role session.

- Customer uses the governed self-registration, authentication and recovery path.
- A verified eligible Customer may directly first-register the Merchant workspace role on the same actor without an existing Store or prior Operator case. This grants no Store right. First admission and active-role re-entry are distinct operations; a disabled, suspended or previously provisioned role cannot self-reactivate through first registration. A Merchant may manage several separately authorized Stores; MARKETPLACE/DIRECT are Store choices, not actor roles.
- For invited Store staff, DSH validates an accepted scoped Store grant before requesting necessary Identity admission. DSH checks whether valid ownership or an accepted grant remains before requesting removal of Merchant workspace eligibility. Grant acceptance and revocation never silently rebind an actor or destroy unrelated authorized roles.
- For Captains, current DSH-approved operational standing precedes authorized Identity role admission and enrollment. A dispatch assignment is neither necessary for standing admission nor a substitute for it. PENDING grants no Captain or delivery authority. An authorized merchant/operations Operator may initiate the appropriate Merchant invitation or Captain candidate; the human proves their own identity. Guest interest is not a standing application or role grant; [fulfillment](fulfillment.md) owns candidate and standing distinctions and admitted entry flows. No separate referral/acquisition role or admission path; preserve lawful historical records at their owners.
- Operator admission follows the protected bootstrap and HR request protocol below. No client, BFF, Store owner, job title or unrelated Operator may create roles or sessions through presentation or local state.

Managed-role requests require current verified domain-owner eligibility and Identity authorization, not phone, caller-selected actor, job or assignment. Revalidate Identity standing after any required peer request; reject races, stale source proof, mismatched actor/role and self-granted managed roles. Any legacy role identifier transition requires a reviewed compatibility contract preserving actor, role and protected history.

## Session lifecycle and public Store identity

Identity issues distinct role-bound session credentials and verifies the current actor, role, expiry, revocation and security versions at the owner. An unavailable owner fails closed. Registration, refresh, suspension, recovery, logout, device change and context switching have explicit authorized transitions. Enrollment alone is not authentication except an expressly admitted combined first-role operation with current verified source proof. Reject repeated activation-as-login. Role disablement revokes the affected role's sessions and prevents their renewal, preserving unrelated roles unless an authorized Identity-wide security action requires otherwise. Refresh rotates atomically; confirmed replay compromises and revokes the affected family. Distinguish a racing duplicate from confirmed replay under the owner contract. A lost response is not permission to recreate a role or revive a revoked credential. Challenges are purpose/expiry/attempt/throttling/single-use bounded; public responses avoid unnecessary account or eligibility disclosure.

One Customer identity and admitted Customer role serve the marketplace and approved public Store websites. Identity owns domain/host-scoped issuance, validation, renewal, logout and revocation. One actor does not imply shared cookies across hosts; a new verification may be required. An admitted cross-domain handoff uses an explicitly reviewed Identity-owned secure flow, never passing access/refresh credentials in Store URLs, cross-origin storage, Merchant-authored scripts or untrusted redirects. A Store owner or staff member uses their separately authorized Merchant workspace, not a public Customer login.

Guests may browse approved public Store pages, offers and prices; offer the canonical Customer sign-in/registration and recovery flow when chosen or required by the admitted operation. A Customer session alone does not admit a Store into marketplace discovery, enable direct checkout, grant Merchant/Operator authority or activate marketplace wallet/Captain/settlement. DSH verifies current Store relation, publication, eligibility and resource authorization for each protected action. Direct checkout and protected Order history remain subject to their distinct commerce, legal and payment contracts; authentication does not itself enable them.

Customer history, protected personal data and Orders are actor- and Store/order-scoped. Merchant/delegated staff receive only data needed for their authorized Store work, never another Store's or marketplace Orders, addresses, balances or session facts. A delivery recipient is a DSH Order contact snapshot, not a purchasing/payment principal or access to Customer wallet/history. Context switches, logout, revoked grants, delayed reads and failure recovery must not expose prior actor/role/Store money, Orders, files or administrative facts. Scope caches, credentials and pending operations to the verified session; owner authorization never trusts caller-authored identity, role, scope, service assertion, headers or UI state. See [safety](safety.md).

Public authentication presentation exposes only actions appropriate to the verified or pending admission state; no decorative protected tabs or simulated disabled workspace navigation. Mount protected navigation only after owner-backed role and required resource-scope checks. Developer inspection must not obstruct authentication or bypass authority. Web/native may adapt layout while retaining accessible admission, action and failure semantics.

## Operator authentication and policy-control admission

Normal Operator login requires user-verified WebAuthn/Passkey, not password-only login or SMS as ordinary MFA. Establish the original privileged Operator once by protected bootstrap. Subsequent Operator admission originates in the canonical company HR access request under [HR law](hr.md); the protected initial privileged Operator alone approves/rejects and authorizes invitation issuance/revocation and selected grants through Identity. HR request creation confers no Operator authority. Before a new Operator receives an invitation, require actual Identity admission and the durable same-actor employee link under HR law. Preserve existing lawfully admitted legacy accounts. Suspended, disabled or already-provisioned Operators cannot self-reactivate through ordinary registration.

Bootstrap, credential enrollment, authentication and recovery are distinct ceremonies. Bind each challenge to its intended operation and approved RP ID/origin, verify challenge, ceremony type, credential/actor binding, applicable cryptographic proof, user presence and required user verification, and reject expired, reused, malformed or mismatched ceremonies. Enrollment/recovery proof is bounded, purpose-specific and single-use. Enrollment or recovery alone issues no Operator session; require a separate successful signed authentication with current actor and role. Serialize bootstrap to one original actor/marker, credential, explicit grants and attributable audit. Recovery preserves the existing actor, role, grants and original-Owner attribution, atomically revokes affected credentials/sessions and invalidates competing claims according to the owner contract. Reject unauthorized re-enrollment and compromised/replayed assertions; enforce actual credential revocation and session invalidation when required. A role suspension invalidates affected sessions and pending invitations; no employment change or automatic restoration of privileges follows. Recovery is controlled break-glass enrollment, not a parallel ordinary login. Root Owner exceptions remain specifically authorized, bounded, attributable and reviewed under the actual risk requirements, never a permanent bypass.

Policy mutation requires a current Identity-authenticated Operator, platform_policies and the applicable additional finance, merchant, operations or other owner scope. DSH/WLT independently verify current principal, scope and attribution at their own boundaries; inability to verify denies the mutation. Menus and BFF checks are defense in depth. Financial execution, approval, reconciliation and read access are distinct authorizations.

## Finite Operator workspace permissions and session hardening

The original bootstrapped Operator is the access administrator and holds the registered scopes. New Operators receive no automatic workspace permission. Only that original active Operator can grant or revoke a registered scope to another currently enabled Operator, with attributable reason, expected version, durable audit and invalidation of affected sessions. Use the current HR request's selected subset for admission review; preserve unrelated permissions and govern subsequent changes through canonical access administration. No scope delegates this administrative power; role alone grants no blanket authority. Verify effective current permission for the relevant authenticated session, not a cached menu or permission read.

| Scope | Bounded authority |
| --- | --- |
| operations | Authorized Order handling and Captain administration; no WLT financial execution |
| merchant | Merchant admission, status and directory; no Store ownership or Store-resource grant |
| catalog | Shared Product/category/attribute administration, catalog proposals and imports under DSH ownership and actual resource scope |
| marketing | Promotions/discovery content; no Identity or financial powers |
| hr | Bounded employee administration and HR audit readback; no Operator admission or permission administration |
| finance | Finance workspace; actual WLT effect requires independent financial authority; no beneficiary redirection without the authorized Store owner decision, no permission administration |
| platform_policies | Owner-backed policy mutation with the applicable additional scope; no change to immutable financial, security or legal invariants |

Leadership remains a read-oriented center, not a new write scope; sensitive reads require actual owner permission. These definitions are not implementation proof.
