## Purpose and Product target

One delivery-commerce platform for Customer, Merchant, Captain and authorized Operator, initially serving approved cities and business types. [Platform](platform.md) owns the actor/surface model; [architecture](architecture.md) owns service and writer boundaries; [journeys](journeys.md) owns observable cross-owner scenarios, not a second Product census.

A Merchant may own multiple canonical DSH Stores. Each has one governed relation under [Store law](stores.md): DIRECT admits only direct commerce; MARKETPLACE additionally admits marketplace commerce/discovery when eligible. Both share the same Store, catalog, cart, Order and fulfillment engines. The full public Store presentation belongs to the same Customer Expo Web app. No extra website framework, merchant-specific wallet or second commerce platform follows from the relation.

## Product invariants

- One Store Order per Store even under multi-Store parent intent; confirmed snapshots and lawful post-confirmation adjustments remain DSH facts. Store relation, publication, availability, Offer stock, serviceability and checkout eligibility are independent.
- Relation, COMMISSION/SUBSCRIPTION fee model, collection responsibility and three fulfillment types (BThwani Captain, Store-affiliated Captain, Customer Pickup) are independently governed under [finance](finance.md) and [fulfillment](fulfillment.md); no policy, badge or selection activates a missing capability.
- Internal wallet, external rail, customer payment allocation, cash custody, Store obligation and beneficiary settlement are distinct. WLT owns money. Subscription entitlements require actual supported capacity/service, exact accepted terms and Finance approval; they grant no roles, ownership, public listing, checkout or guaranteed payment rail.
- Merchant is an actor and Store a resource; bounded DSH HR covers company and Merchant personnel without another service, generic payroll or automatic Identity privileges. Recipient differs from purchaser where admitted, without gaining purchasing/payment authority.
- Private Order communication/media, transactional notifications, catalog imports, promotions, bounded operations, Captain opportunities, financial reports, multi-Store Merchant work, secure links and Order-scoped feedback/incident/refund are material subfeatures. Include them in owner source reconciliation without creating redundant standalone Products.
- Presentation and derived reads are not mutation authority. Success requires actual committed owner readback. Missing admitted breadth is not automatically a defect outside the authorized delivery scope.

## Exclusions and scope change

Current non-goals: standalone loyalty, social reviews/community, generic support/ticketing, advanced route optimization, generic analytics or notification Product, broad customer-profile/privacy orchestration, generic Merchant organization/team beyond bounded Store grants, generic multi-tenant SaaS beyond admitted Store Web hosting, ERP/POS replacement, speculative multi-currency, scheduled Customer Orders, point-to-point courier requests, generic returns/exchanges, electronics warranty claims, paid Customer membership, mandatory daily Store check-in and predictive/numeric Store capacity. Prescription-required or specially regulated pharmacy fulfillment requires explicit legal/operational admission; ordinary lawful pharmacy commerce does not activate it.

These exclusions describe the current approved Product, not a ban on authorized isolated development. Under [progressive admission](README.md#progressive-capability-admission--2026-11-14-human-approval), new proposals may be analyzed, specified and implemented in bounded development without a separate preliminary vote. Actual Product adoption requires demonstrated need, a distinct responsibility not better owned by an existing capability, one owner/writer/readback, actors/surfaces, contracts, authorization, privacy/legal scope, failure/recovery and measurable acceptance. Resolve consequential commercial/legal choices with their actual authority; no implicit binding terms or production activation. Changing a supported configurable default is not a new capability. The original 26 IDs are traceability, not a maximum.

Archive, competitor and OSS existence are research, not automatic approval. Do not revive retired acquisition/onboarding roles, enrollment surfaces or reward writers. Preserve independently verified binding historical liabilities with their actual owners.

## Progressive commercial intelligence within admitted capabilities

Improve existing actor work through owner-backed catalog quality/history, availability, discovery, Store readiness, content, factual metrics and actionable exceptions while stabilizing Orders, fulfillment and finance. No standalone analytics Product or fourth service. Apply [measurement law](architecture.md#measurement-integrity-and-intelligence-admission). Completed sales, contribution and settlement require qualifying DSH/WLT facts. Forecasting, replenishment, advanced personalization, predictive dispatch, assistants, supplier purchasing and new commercial models require demonstrated need, adequate data, scope and actual acceptance; independent ready increments need not wait for unrelated work. Hypothetical results do not prove implementation or release.
