## EMPLOYEE_HR — Employee records and approved workplace duties
CAPABILITY_ID: EMPLOYEE_HR

**Purpose:** One bounded DSH `employees` engine may manage approved employment records for both BThwani company staff and each Merchant's staff: organizational ownership, optional links to the one Identity actor, job/department assignments, employment documents and individually admitted attendance or custody workflows. A Merchant employment record is not a BThwani company employment record. No fourth service, parallel Merchant HR engine, generic payroll or globally privileged employee role is admitted by this placement decision.

DSH owns approved employee facts and the single bounded HR implementation; Identity owns the human account, sessions and platform permissions; DSH separately owns Store membership/delegated permissions and operational actions; WLT alone owns financial postings. An employee may have no platform account, or may have one Identity actor linked to assignments across multiple authorized Stores without duplicate identities. Creating employment, assigning a department or associating a Store **never** grants platform or Store access automatically. Every employment record retains one stable organization-scoped employee identifier, attributable job history, reporting line and employment start/end state; a record links to at most one canonical Identity actor without manufacturing a second person or a cross-employer employment relationship.

BThwani personnel data and each Merchant's personnel data require separate organizational scopes, least-privilege company/Store permissions, privacy-safe audit, lawful retention and safe separation. An authorized Merchant may administer only their eligible organization/Store staff under DSH grants; company HR Operators require explicit Identity scopes. No cross-Merchant or Merchant-to-company employee visibility is implied. Operator/Merchant screens invoke DSH rather than writing a second employment registry.

Attendance and compensation are independently admitted when required and legally supported; do not invent payroll, generic ERP, unrestricted personnel tracking, or financial writer from historic spreadsheets or screenshots.

**Recovery/proof:** Two employee accounts with different rights, revoked role, historical employment event, attachment/PII boundaries, failed write, idempotent retry and exact owner readback. Do not confuse a working HR card with payroll readiness.

## Employee admission versus Operator identity

Employment lifecycle and account/role lifecycle are separate. An authorized HR Operator may request Operator access for an eligible employee, but the **initial bootstrapped privileged Operator** owns normal Operator-admission approval and scoped permission grants under the current protected Identity policy. An employee may have no platform account; merely being a Captain or Merchant actor does not establish employment. A Store employee is staff of the specific Merchant organization when explicitly appointed, not automatically BThwani company staff. Existing employee Identity is reused, never duplicated under an HR-only login table.

DSH holds authorized employee employment terms, attendance, documents and relevant custody with organization-scoped history; WLT alone records authorized actual money movements and reconciled liabilities. No global Merchant payroll or unapproved company compensation writer is introduced. A designation or department cannot grant Finance, Store, Captain dispatch or Root Owner privileges. Private HR media/documents are not public marketing assets. An HR entitlement or approved salary calculation is not a WLT payout, and employee salary disbursement must not inherit Captain or Merchant earnings rules. Monetary cash advances reconcile only through WLT; physical equipment issuance and returns remain attributable DSH employee-custody facts. Historical payroll approvals and ledger evidence are not deleted to simplify the new employee registry.

## HR access-review and employee lifecycle safety

An HR-authorized employee record may be created without a platform login. An HR employee access request contains *proposed* Operator permissions only; only the protected initial privileged Operator can approve **or reject** the request, issue/revoke Operator invitations and grant an explicit subset through Identity. HR does not write Identity permissions. A new Operator admission originates from a canonical HR access request; rejected requests remain auditable and may be corrected then resubmitted, but a request already claimed for Identity actor creation cannot be rejected without a safe reconciliation outcome. Record the Identity-approved canonical actor link on the same employee only after durable owner readback; retry incomplete Identity-to-HR writeback by one operation identity without duplicate actors or silently losing a reviewed rejection. Existing admitted legacy Operator accounts remain protected and readable rather than automatically deleted or converted. Root Owner direct-development access does not relax production passkey/enrollment constraints.

The employee access review uses **current live Identity permission readback** to grant or revoke only the selected subset requested by HR with an attributed decision; unchanged permissions must not be rewritten on a retry, unrelated permissions are preserved, and affected Operator sessions are revoked after a real change. HR request IDs remain correlation evidence, never a second permission authority. Suspending an Operator role leaves the employee's HR employment state unchanged; reinstating that role requires separate protected Identity authorization and an active employee. A linked actor ID alone is not proof of currently enabled Control Panel access. The Operator workspace composes one DSH-backed HR directory and employee creation/readback path, not an independent Identity employee editor.

The initial manual attendance flow distinguishes explicit presence, absence, lateness and leave **annotations**. A leave annotation is not a leave approval or a payroll instruction. An unrecorded day is **unknown, not absent**. A correction requires expected record version, reason and append-only attributable HR event. Overnight intervals may be recorded, but attendance and biometric evidence are not automatic salary deductions or leave approvals. DSH owns approved employment terms and entitlement, WLT owns actual financial posting/settlement, and equipment custody and cash advance are distinct obligations.

Employee history is append-only, reasoned, attributed and time-stamped at DSH; only current HR-read permission can access bounded paginated audit events. General staff directory reads must not expose compensation destinations, sensitive employee attachments, or unrelated Merchant/company records. Verified biometric/device attendance is optional evidence, not a payroll event, and biometric templates are not copied into an ordinary HR database.

When ending employment of an active employee with Operator access, Identity must first disable that role and revoke sessions/invitations, then HR marks employment inactive with attributable replay-safe write. If the second owner is unavailable, keep the user safely disabled and reconcile HR later; **restarting employment never restores old privileges automatically**. For an employee linked to an active Operator role, the protected initial Operator/Root Owner must authorize the Identity disabling step before HR commits inactive employment; an HR-authorized operator may deactivate only a non-Operator employee under the admitted policy. An active manager with reports cannot leave active management until reporting relationships are resolved. Preserve employee record history without deleting old personal or accounting facts merely to clean a directory. Employment-state changes retain their attributable reason and before/after HR facts in the same append-only, HR-read-only paginated audit, not a second audit ledger.
