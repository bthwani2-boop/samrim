## EMPLOYEE_HR — Employee records and approved workplace duties
CAPABILITY_ID: EMPLOYEE_HR

DSH owns one bounded employees engine for authorized BThwani company and Merchant employment records: employer, stable employee ID, optional canonical Identity actor link, job and department history, reporting line, documents, employment start/end and admitted attendance or custody. An employment record belongs to one verified employer; a manager must be active in that organization. A person may have separate lawful employment relationships with different employers and one canonical Identity actor; each record links to at most one actor, optionally none. Store affiliation is neither employment nor permission. Captain, Merchant or Store staff status does not establish BThwani employment without an explicit independent employment relationship. HR contact phone may be absent; enrollment phone requires Identity verification. Do not merge employer histories through global contact or actor uniqueness; retained historical identity/uniqueness migration requires protected compatible review.

Identity alone owns accounts, roles, invitations, sessions and platform permissions. DSH owns employment and separately Store grants; WLT alone posts monetary effects. No fourth service, separate Merchant HR registry, HR-only login, generic payroll or globally privileged employee role.

Company and each Merchant HR remain isolated. Authorized Merchant manages only eligible organization/Store staff under DSH grants; company HR requires explicit Identity scope. Job, department, seniority, reporting line and employment confer no Store, dispatch, Finance, Operator or Root Owner authority. Directory and mutation authorization are independently enforced at DSH. Compensation, payout destinations and private employment documents need dedicated narrower authority and are not general-directory data; audit access follows current scope. No public marketing use of private HR media, cross-employer visibility or frontend policy engine. Non-login employee creation must not depend on optional financial or biometric data.

### Registration and interrupted operation recovery

Approved 2026-11-25: company and Merchant registration use the same public DSH client protocol for normalized validation, operation identity, opaque recovery, serialized claims, identical-request replay and outcome classification. Distinct authorized routes and Identity checks remain. Apps own only platform presentation, storage/crypto/lock adapter and authenticated transport. Namespace distinguishes verified actor and employer (company vs Merchant), not individual Store affiliation.

Before transmission persist operation identity and payload fingerprint, not raw employee fields or bearer. Denial, conflict or uncertainty never authorizes a replacement unresolved identity; the same authorized actor re-enters original values. Confirmed or definitive initial rejection may settle attempt; retain one opaque last-generation marker against stale concurrent tabs. Storage/lock failure blocks new submission. Local recovery metadata is no employee/permission truth; actual DSH authorization, immutable replay and durable owner readback decide outcome.

## Employee admission versus Operator identity

An HR-authorized Operator may submit an access request only for an eligible company employee. The protected initial privileged Operator alone approves/rejects, authorizes invitation issuance/revocation and grants the selected permission subset through [Identity](identity.md#operator-authentication-and-policy-control-admission). HR, Store grants and request creation confer no Operator access. New Operator admission originates in the canonical DSH HR access request. Preserve lawfully admitted legacy accounts; direct-development access never relaxes Identity enrollment/passkey constraints.

The request preserves proposed permissions and immutable submission-time name, verified contact claim, department and job snapshot. Later employee edits cannot rewrite the request or expand the selected grants. Non-login employees without verified phone remain legitimate; HR contact alone is not invitation proof. Rejection remains auditable; HR may correct and resubmit. A request already claimed for Identity actor creation cannot be rejected without resolving its partial effect. Before a new Operator receives an invitation, prove actual Identity role admission and durably write back the same canonical actor link to the same employee. Retry incomplete writeback under the original operation identity; an unknown or failed link blocks invitation issuance until reconciled. Never duplicate an actor, silently lose a reviewed rejection or grant another employer access.

The protected initial Operator reviews live Identity permission state; approve or revoke only a selected subset of the request with attributable reason. HR request ID is correlation, not permission truth. Unchanged grants are not rewritten on retry; preserve unrelated permissions and use separate canonical access administration for permissions outside the request. Identity invalidates affected sessions on real change. Role suspension revokes active Operator sessions and pending Operator invitations without changing employment. Reinstatement requires protected Identity authorization and active employment. A linked actor is not evidence of enabled Control Panel access. Operator uses one DSH-backed employee directory, creation and readback, not a second Identity employee editor.

## Employment lifecycle, attendance and custody

HR-authorized Operators may explicitly record dated presence, absence, lateness or leave annotation for active employees in authorized scope. Unknown/unrecorded day is not absence. Leave annotation is not approval or payroll instruction. Corrections require expected version, reason and append-only attributed event; preserve every version, actor, correlation and timestamp. Optional overnight intervals do not infer lateness or salary deductions. Verified device attendance, if admitted, is attributable, chronological and idempotent evidence, not salary authority; missing device data is not absence. Do not centrally copy biometric templates as ordinary HR data. Shift planning, leave approval, compensation and device integration require separate bounded lawful admission.

DSH owns employment terms, approved salary entitlement calculation and physical equipment issue/return. WLT alone owns actual financial posting, payout, external transfer, cash advance settlement and reconciled liabilities. Salary does not inherit Captain/Merchant earnings rules. No generic payroll/ERP or speculative personnel tracking; retain historical payroll approvals and ledger evidence.

DSH retains append-only creation, employment changes and status history with reason, actor, timestamp and before/after employment facts. Only current scoped HR-read permission admits bounded paginated audit; general interface exposes necessary metadata, not sensitive compensation, destinations, attachments or unrelated organization facts. Lawful retention and protected deletion follow [safety](safety.md).

Ending employment for an active Operator requires protected initial Operator/Root Owner authorization, actual Identity role disablement and session/invitation revocation with durable readback before DSH commits inactive status. If DSH is unavailable, keep Operator access safely disabled and retry the same attributed operation idempotently. HR-authorized Operator may deactivate only a non-Operator employee under admitted policy. An active manager with reports cannot leave active management until those relationships are resolved. Reactivation never automatically restores prior privileges or sessions. Preserve identity, employee record and prior accounting facts; use the same append-only audit, not a second ledger.
