## Financial integrity and distinct dimensions

Identity owns actor/access; DSH owns Store, relation/channel, commercial quote, Order and fulfillment; WLT exclusively owns internal money, funding, allocation/holds, ledger, agreements, fee attribution, receivables and settlement. Authoritative amounts are server-derived from trusted owner facts and versioned policy. Client totals, screenshots, provider labels, direct SQL, shadow Store ledger and alternate wallet/billing engines are not financial truth.

YER amounts are exact integers; amount_minor = one YER. No authoritative binary floating-point arithmetic. WLT applies versioned rounding to amount × rate_bps / 10,000. Every monetary effect must balance, be authorized, attributable, immutable in history, correlated, concurrency-safe, idempotent and canonically readable. DSH proves the qualifying transition; durable handoff and WLT posting are separate, not a distributed atomic transaction. Reconcile unknown/partial outcomes using the same logical identity and payload before another effect.

Four independent dimensions:
- Store relation and Order sales channel are separate DSH facts under [stores](stores.md) and [commerce](commerce.md).
- WLT fee model COMMISSION or SUBSCRIPTION is separately accepted and Finance-approved for either relation.
- WLT collector PLATFORM_MANAGED or STORE_MANAGED is separately accepted for either relation; never infer it from relation or fee model. Older PLATFORM_COLLECTION/MERCHANT_COLLECTION are historical meanings, not alternative live enums.
- DSH fulfillment method is independently eligible subject to current financial, operational and legal readiness.

CustomerPaymentAllocation answers how the customer's payable amount is funded (admitted wallet/cash); SettlementAllocation answers how entitlement/funding is distributed (Store proceeds, commission, Captain earnings, discounts, subsidies, refunds and adjustments). Platform-funded discount is a funding source, not another copy of merchandise value.

### Captain physical COD and exposure

In the approved initial BThwani Captain model the independent Captain keeps physical Customer cash. BThwani does not accept new physical remittance from Captains or Merchants, or assume physical disbursement to them. A Captain's exchanger deposit is external wallet balance until verified Cash-In. Store-affiliated Captains and Store-collected cash are not platform Captain custody.

At exact atomic assignment/acceptance of a positive COD/mixed cash portion, available unheld coverage = posted Captain WLT balance minus active COD holds, payout holds and other governed locks. Approved initial eligibility requires this cover strictly greater than complete confirmed Order payable (products and delivery), not merely cash portion. Atomically reserve 100% of actual positive cash portion; concurrent reservations cannot reuse cover. Prepaid-only Orders have no COD coverage requirement; prepaid Customer Wallet portion is never debited from Captain again. On confirmed collection/delivery by the assigned Captain, WLT posts applicable delivery entitlement and debits reserved cash against receivable once (DEBITED). No subsequent same-cash remittance. Cancellation before collection releases hold without debit. Goods loss needs separately bounded contractual controls; cash hold alone is not loss compensation.

Historical initial activation funding reference is 10,000 YER, checked only before first availability, not midnight reset or mandatory daily top-up. Previously activated Captain may take prepaid Orders subject to identity, standing and goods-custody risk. Validate quantitative adequacy and concurrency before activation; changes to threshold require explicit future-effective WLT policy and evidence, preserving frozen Orders. Historical FINALIZED/REMITTED are actual old states, not automatically DEBITED or subject to second charge.

### Store-collected proceeds and commission receivable

For PARTNER_CAPTAIN cash, affiliated Captain transfers collected proceeds to Store and DSH records collection, handoff and acknowledgment; WLT creates no platform Captain COD receivable, wallet movement or earning from those proceeds. Outstanding eligible commission receivables reduce only the same liable Merchant's lawfully eligible earnings before payout under accepted Store-specific attribution and authorized offset; never another Merchant's entitlement or unapproved cross-Store offset. WLT clears by verified receipt/remittance, lawful offset, reversal or period settlement; verify payer, destination, amount, balance, statement/counterparty and exact source. Merchant report, uploaded image, closed case or ambiguous provider result does not prove settlement. Duplicate reference, mismatch, overpayment, stale account, timeout and refund-after-payment stay reconcilable. Period/due policy cannot rewrite accepted terms. See PARTNER_COMMISSION_REMITTANCE in [journeys](journeys.md).

### Delivery tariff, markup and promotions

For eligible Store-affiliated delivery, Store chooses admitted free, fixed or distance-based delivery fee under versioned terms; economic owner and collection responsibility are frozen per Order. No platform Captain wage, exposure or margin inferred. Pickup has no platform Captain fee/custody.

The donor-approved initial BTHWANI_CAPTAIN combination permits a fixed Customer delivery markup on standard distance tariff only for an eligible zero-product-commission Store with exact accepted WLT terms. Customer sees full fee before confirmation. WLT freezes base tariff, markup and agreement; reject mismatched markup or application to another mode. Captain earns entire base distance tariff; distinct markup is platform delivery-margin income (historical PLATFORM_DELIVERY_MARGIN_INCOME), not reduced Captain entitlement or disguised product commission. Nonzero commission plus markup or another stacking combination requires separate approved economics/conservation and actual owner proof. WLT accounts for exact frozen promotion funding split without duplicate charge or reinterpretation.

### Refunds and compensation

DSH owns lawful cancellation/adjustment under [commerce](commerce.md), including frozen Store-Type rule, actual stage, consent and permitted actor. Technical checkout rollback is not commercial cancellation. WLT releases original reservation/cash obligation before collection, not new credit. A qualifying accepted OrderAdjustment records one durable WLT reconciliation case; cancelled payment intent cannot create a new case. Before collection a pending case is not authority to reprice, collect more, refund, credit or proceed through incompatible fulfillment. Block incompatible transition until authorized WLT repricing/cancellation readback. After collection a new balanced delta or reversal preserves the original snapshot; prove collected payment, approved amount, funding source, remaining entitlement and affected Store/commission allocation. Additional value requires lawful WLT authorization, never assumed by Merchant/DSH.

Verified eligible Customer credit returns to the existing WLT Customer Wallet by default, spendable at eligible Stores, not a separate refund writer or promise of money. No automatic compensation, guessed credit, duplicate reimbursement or silent reduction of Store earnings. Exceptional external return follows authorized manual Operations/Finance transfer, receipt and independent reconciliation; no platform physical cash handover or duplicate credit. Any actual Store-custody cash return must follow its accepted legal collector obligation and reconcile, not revive platform cash routes.

Narrow admitted platform-funded compensation: confirmed-collected, Customer-approved full-item removal on an already completed Order with no promotion/discount. DSH derives amount from immutable line; authorized Finance reviews, WLT checks collected evidence and caps total credits to original line amount. Post audited platform expense versus Customer Wallet liability once per adjustment; DSH closes only after actual WLT ledger and wallet readback. Not reversal of settled Merchant proceeds. An APPLIED removal cannot yield second compensation for that line. Other quantity, Store-funded, promotional, pre-collection or disputed cases remain open until correct reconciliation. Deny adjustment/cancellation/collection races, insufficient amount and duplicate case/effect; unknown collection remains unknown.
