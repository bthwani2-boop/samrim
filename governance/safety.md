## Security and privacy are invariants, not policy switches

Identity establishes trusted actor and service authority; every protected action and object is independently authorized at its canonical owner against the current authenticated principal, action, resource, role and applicable Store, city or delegated scope. No caller-authored identity, role, ownership or service assertion establishes authority. Missing or invalid trusted context fails closed. Root Owner restrictions, tenant isolation, CSRF/IDOR defenses, ledger conservation, audit, retained rights, lawful consent and privacy are not adjustable policy choices.

Authentication and recovery avoid unnecessary enumeration. Bound security-sensitive challenges by purpose, expiry, attempts, throttling, single use and replay protection. Protect credentials, tokens and recovery material by purpose and scope, rotate or revoke on compromise and never log secrets. Operator authentication follows [Identity](identity.md), never a weaker parallel login.

Collect personal data only for a defined lawful purpose, minimize it and expose or redact it according to materially authorized need, including precise location, financial, Store, Order, media, Customer and recipient facts. Public URLs, cursors, offline storage, pending requests or stale caches cannot admit draft/unapproved Stores, unrelated actors, candidate Captains or revoked memberships. Only the lawfully assigned Captain receives the private delivery details needed for that task.

Company HR and each Merchant HR remain isolated; employment or Store association confers no implicit Identity or Store authority. Deny unrelated personnel, private attachments, stale sessions and revoked staff scopes. WLT alone records monetary effects; [HR](hr.md) owns employment and its bounded custody/attendance.

## Location consent and current-mission least privilege

DSH may retain only the bounded latest availability fix needed for eligible platform dispatch; no historical movement trail or individual-Captain directory. Before acceptance, candidate and aggregate maps conceal precise Customer destination, private address and individual Captain location.

An admitted active assignment may separately expose its single latest mission fix, bounded by freshness, accuracy and sequence, only to specifically authorized Operations for that Order. Customer, Merchant, other Captains, general notifications and opportunity maps have no inherited GPS entitlement. Assignment end, expiry, revocation, logout and lawful retention cleanup end effective access. Stale/offline observations remain explicit; never fabricate movement.

Android background tracking requires affirmative platform permission and a visible operating notification where required. JavaScript export is not proof of native consent or behavior. [Fulfillment](fulfillment.md) owns operational thresholds and mission semantics within these privacy constraints.

## Policy mutation protections

[Architecture](architecture.md#safe-change-protocol) owns the canonical mutation and rollback protocol, not a second settings authority. Prove rejection of unauthorized actor/scope, invalid or stale version, incompatible concurrent write and unsafe replay after unknown outcome. A configured value cannot activate unsupported billing, payment, publication, dispatch, cash or legally unaccepted behavior. No permissive stale-client fallback or rollback that erases accepted rights, agreements or history.

## SQL, finance and migration protection

WLT alone owns authoritative wallets and ledgers. Preserve exact integer YER arithmetic, double-entry conservation, immutable history and once-only release/debit/settlement under retry and concurrency. Disputed and uncertain effects remain explicit, never manufactured by direct SQL or test fixtures.

Each durable fact follows its single canonical writer and owning migration history. [SQL-01](engineering.md#sql-01--safe-one-baseline-per-owner-fresh-bootstrap-protected-upgrades) owns baseline-first, fresh schema and retained-upgrade lifecycle; [repository structure](../REPOSITORY-STRUCTURE.md) owns placement. A fresh baseline is not a retained-data migration. Schema and application consumers cut over coherently; dual-write or shadow coexistence is not a second authority and is allowed only for a separately proven necessary bounded transition under the owning migration contract, with reconciliation and removal evidence. Do not authorize destructive reset because a tree is clean or a database is labeled development. Preserve actual applied history, checksums, records, sessions, grants, Root Owner, Orders, balances, audit, media, credentials and shared data. No new retained-data epoch without separate approval and rehearsed compatible upgrade.

Before protected migration or deletion, verify exact source, applied schema/version/checksums, affected records, preconditions, authority and restricted restorable backup. Rehearse the same deterministic ordered migration and restore on an isolated copy. Coordinate or stop incompatible writers under actual authority; reject mismatching version, ownership, preconditions or backup integrity. Apply through the owning migration runner. Before resuming writers, prove applied version, preserved identities, constraints, grants, financial/session invariants and compatible owner-service durable readback. Passing fresh bootstrap never certifies retained migration or restore.

Synthetic writes require verified database/namespace, role, isolation and operation-specific admission. Never use production credentials, real customer contacts or live payment/message rails. Separate automated tests from persistent development and each other. Keep test credentials isolated; dispose only after verified ownership, absence of retained/shared dependency and authorization. Preserve useful and unknown-origin development data; no test-only business columns or alternate writers. New Docker/Compose test environments use distinct project/services, networks, ports and volumes, never implicit replacement of an existing environment. No shared reset through normal development lifecycle. See [local data lifecycle](engineering.md#local-development-data-lifecycle).

## Software and supply-chain safeguards

Preserve proven dependencies, signing identities, domain routes and credential boundaries. New plugin, wrapper, CI agent, secret store, provider or dependency needs distinct demonstrated responsibility and necessity. Use official compatible versions, locked dependencies, verified source/artifacts and applicable vulnerability/security checks; no hidden substitution or weakened acceptance.

Each service or platform connection has one explicit effective owner configuration contract per trust boundary. Authenticate the actual destination, preserve TLS and hostname verification, bound requests/responses and timeouts, and reject unauthorized redirects, origins, hosts and implicit fallbacks. Do not let an ambient HTTP proxy or other intermediary receive credentials, authority-bearing requests or private owner facts unless distinct security necessity, actual destination and specific authorization are established and the protected transport is proven. Prefer the original owner's direct authenticated interface and native transport. CORS, routing and UI state are not authorization. Never expose private credentials or tokens through a browser bundle, public environment variable, log, URL or weaker client persistence.

Removing a Proxy, BFF, Adapter, Relay or other intermediary must preserve actual session/revocation semantics, credential isolation, TLS, CSRF, CSP, owner authorization, audit, bounded I/O, cancellation, concurrency, idempotency and durable recovery as applicable. Transfer each necessary protection to its original owner or required native trust boundary before removing the losing path; do not merely rename it, replace it with a competing intermediary or strip protection to achieve direct access. Test authorized use and rejected foreign destination, redirect, stale actor/scope, forged request and interrupted/replayed effect as applicable. If a security responsibility cannot yet be transferred safely, keep its affected transition open and admit no weaker operation. The placement owner defines structural necessity, not a blanket exemption for anything named security.

GitHub Actions use minimum token permissions and must not expose secrets to untrusted PR/fork code. Required checks and provider reviews are verification instruments, not authority to approve business decisions. Verify candidate credential filenames and bytes at the actual publication boundary without logging values; checking only working bytes cannot certify staged or outgoing committed content. Preserve peer isolation and existing canonical reader safety; use the existing security owner and native immutable Git evidence, not a second secret detector. Retired features cannot regain production access; retained legal or financial liabilities stay protected at their owners, without restoring an obsolete writer or screen for new transactions.

## Proof, internal technical risk assurance and incident readiness

For affected critical risks, prove actual owner-backed denial and durable outcomes: forged actor, cross-Store and cross-Captain access/IDOR, cross-account switch with pending reads, stale/revoked grants, policy races, price/cart change, COD/Mixed partial funding, double debit, settlement timeout, refund, lost/late dispatch acceptance, publication rollback and media privacy. Include interruption, recovery, concurrency and applicable restoration proof. Missing tests remain NOT_RUN or REVIEW_BLOCKED, never PASS.

**Human-approved 2026-11-26 risk assurance:** The responsible executor performs attributable internal adversarial assessment and verifies evidence. No separate independent reviewer or reviewer signature is mandatory merely for development or acceptance, including financial implementation. Before activating actual finance, authentication/security, protected migration/deletion or sensitive-data effects, require applicable specific human approval, owner contracts and authorization, negative verification, financial/concurrency or restore proof and documented risk assessment. Unverified implementation remains unaccepted. Self-assertions, documentation, a green pipeline or HTTP 200 are not substitutes. Do not represent external audit or signature as performed; legal consent, obligations and actual protected authority remain binding.

## Database roles, enforceable constraints and bounded reads

Each service's database runtime identity has only the minimum privileges necessary for its owned operations, including on a shared server. A serving identity is never a superuser or schema administrator, cannot acquire those powers through membership or object ownership, and receives no unnecessary role/database creation, replication, RLS bypass, schema creation or grant authority. Administration and migrations use distinct controlled identities. A controlled owner-side command may expose only its explicitly admitted operation and scoped authority, not general elevated SQL. Cross-owner reads require a justified explicit reviewed contract or approved bounded projection, never indiscriminate SQL. Prove the actual connected principal, effective privileges, permitted same-owner effects and denial of unrelated-owner reads/writes and unauthorized elevation.

Enforce expressible uniqueness, foreign keys, NOT NULL, range/check constraints, atomicity, concurrency/idempotency and required scope/authorization at the strongest practical persistence boundary. Client/service validation complements that boundary and never replaces it. New or modified source, records and transport payloads use verified UTF-8 without BOM; decode/transcode legacy external encodings only at ingestion. Preserve Arabic Unicode, mixed-direction identifiers and original facts without guessed decoding or mojibake; verify API/storage/display round trips when affected. A justified consumer-required BOM (such as Excel-compatible CSV) is an export exception, not a source convention.

Generated and derived read state is rebuildable, not mutation authority. Growing collections use owner-side bounded pagination/filter/sort and deterministic order with a stable tie-breaker and permission-scoped cursors where needed. Reject stale or mismatched scope without leaking another Store or silently changing the query. Choose the simplest correct paging/incremental mechanism at the owner; replace it if measured cost degrades materially beyond its operating envelope while preserving canonical result semantics, not through a universal paging engine.

## Failure contract, cross-owner degraded state and evidence

Every material mutation defines owner retry/idempotency and stable machine-classifiable error categories with canonical operation/reconciliation identities. Human-readable Arabic text is not a business-decision parser. Concurrency-sensitive ownership, assignment, custody and terminal transitions fail deterministically without forking truth. A multi-record command unable to remain safely bounded as one synchronous atomic operation has a finite identifiable scope, resumable progress and reconstructable durable per-item outcomes; never label partial progress an atomic success.

A missing second-owner acknowledgment in a money or authorization handoff is explicitly incomplete/degraded. Local success or historical snapshot cannot alone authorize an irreversible transfer; dependent financial execution remains blocked until current persisted eligibility is revalidated. Reconcile unknown provider or cross-service results before a potentially duplicate effect. Reconstruct restart/resume from current owner facts, not stale memory or cached client success; recovery requires final owner readback. A timeout changes durable state only when the domain contract defines that consequence. Offline/degraded UI never fabricates canonical success; no silent fallback to a different owner, writer, truth or success semantics. A lawful alternate path must remain explicitly bounded by the owning capability and observable as alternate/degraded. Health/readiness distinguish configured, available, degraded and unknown where material; correlate causes without leaking secrets or unnecessary personal data.

Media has one lawful upload/reference/retention path, one storage owner and durable reference model when BThwani owns that lifecycle. Replacement/removal preserves referential integrity; remove superseded or orphaned media only after ownership, absence of retained/shared dependency, lawful retention and authorization are proved. A derived preview cannot make private Store/Order attachments public. The actual fact owner executes lawful retention, deletion and anonymization without erasing another owner's protected history; provenance, licensing and visual use remain with the relevant design/domain owner.
