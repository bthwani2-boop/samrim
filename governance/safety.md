## Security and privacy are invariants, not policy switches
Identity authenticates the actor; the real DSH/WLT owner authorizes each protected mutation and read by role, current Store, city, delegated scope and action. Operator Policies can configure **eligible business choices**, never bypass actor validation, Root Owner restrictions, CSRF, IDOR protection, ledger conservation, tenant isolation, audit trail, protected-data retention or legal consent.

Authentication, recovery and machine-to-machine access require trusted proof, never caller-authored actor IDs, roles or service authority; missing or invalid context fails closed. Public identity/recovery responses avoid unnecessary account or role enumeration. Security-sensitive challenges explicitly bound expiry, attempts, throttling and one-time/replay use. Tokens, credentials and recovery secrets are purpose-bound, protected, rotated or revoked on compromise and excluded from logs. Operator access follows Identity rather than a parallel weaker login; negative tests must cover cross-actor, cross-Store and cross-Captain boundaries.

All financial, Store, Order, media and Customer/recipient data must respect least privilege and need-to-know. Draft or unapproved Stores, unrelated actors, candidate Captains and revoked members do not gain access through a public URL, cursor, mobile offline storage or stale session-scoped request. A Captain receives private customer delivery details only when validly assigned for that operation. Store-facing media links and customer contact data need bounded exposure and lawful retention.

The single DSH employee-record engine must enforce a company-versus-Merchant organization boundary and isolation between Merchants. Any employee-to-Store association or employment lifecycle change is separate from Identity login grants and DSH Store grants; neither grants the other by implication. Deny cross-organization HR records, private attachments, stale sessions and revoked staff scopes with accountable owner-backed readback. WLT remains the only financial writer.

## Location consent and current-mission least privilege

For platform Captain dispatch, DSH may retain only a current bounded **latest availability fix** sufficient to rank eligible candidates, not a historical route trail or a customer-visible Captain directory. Pre-acceptance candidate and aggregate maps must conceal precise Customer destination, Store-independent private address and every individual Captain location. Actual assigned-mission location, if admitted, is latest-only, time/accuracy/sequence bounded and readable exclusively by the specifically authorized Operations role for the active Order. No Customer, Merchant, other Captain, general notification or opportunity map may inherit mission GPS access. Ending the assignment, expiry/revocation, logout and approved retention cleanup remove effective access; stale/offline status is displayed honestly, never fabricated movement. Any Android background mission tracking requires affirmative platform permissions and a valid visible operating notification where required; it may not be inferred from an Expo JavaScript export.

## Policy mutation protections
- Every editable policy has one owner, permitted scope and options, authenticated actor/permission, canonical version, effective date, reason, audit log, idempotent mutation and owner-backed readback.
- Two concurrent changes to the same scope must not both silently succeed with contradictory effective versions. A timed-out POST cannot be retried with a fresh mutation identity until canonical read resolves the outcome.
- A stored policy is not proof an unimplemented capability can activate. Owner checks billing, payment, publication, dispatch/cash and legal readiness before applying it to a real request.
- Rollback is a new version and preserves prior agreements, Order/customer rights and financial history. Every policy read and write has explicit failure behavior; never fall back to a permissive stale client constant.

## SQL, finance and migration protection
- WLT is sole authoritative wallet/ledger owner. Preserve exact integer YER money arithmetic, double-entry conservation, immutable transaction history, once-only release/debit/settlement under retry and concurrency; disputed or unknown outcomes stay explicit.
- Each service owner has one canonical migration/source of schema truth. A new clean baseline is designed by reasoning from supported capabilities, not copying historic SQL. Applied migration history and actual databases must not be silently squashed or rewritten; isolate fresh bootstrap and protected upgrade/rollback/restore proof.
- **Protected migration preflight and replay (branch-41 safety continuity):** Record the exact source and applied schema version, migration checksums, existing records and incompatible preconditions before changing any retained local, shared or production-like state. Obtain the required authorization and a **verified, restricted, restorable backup**; rehearse the same ordered deterministic migration and restore on an isolated copy. Coordinate or stop incompatible writers before the owner migration runner changes protected schema, and fail closed if versions, preconditions, ownership or backup integrity do not match. After migration, prove the applied schema/version, record identities, constraints, privileges, financial and session invariants, and compatible owner-service durable readback **before resuming writers**. A passing fresh bootstrap or disposable test never authorizes rewriting applied history, dropping protected data, or changing the production database; the user-approved 2026-11-13 process requires documented internal security/finance/migration assurance and real protected restore evidence before sensitive application; no external reviewer signature is assumed or required merely to approve target policy, and unrun technical evidence is not `PASS`.
- Protect Root Owner, users, sessions, wallet balances, Orders, media, provider secrets, schemas and shared environment data. No direct SQL/test fixture may manufacture financial or security PASS. Test credentials and synthetic data need authorization/isolation and secure disposal.
- A new Docker/Compose environment uses separately named project/services, networks, ports and volumes. Do not start or replace containers in the old environment merely to assemble governance.

## Software and supply-chain safeguards
- Retain proven dependencies, signing identities, domain routes and credential boundaries. Do not add a plugin, wrapper, CI agent, secret store or external provider without a concrete owner and necessity.
- GitHub Actions use minimum token permissions and do not expose secrets to untrusted PR/fork code. Fast Guard and provider review checks are proof instruments, not authority to approve business decisions.
- Retired historical features do not regain production permissions. If historic financial/legal liabilities survive, preserve them in protected owner history without restoring a screen or writer for new transactions.

## Proof, independent review and incident readiness
For affected risk surfaces: forged actor/Store IDOR, account switch with pending reads, stale permissions, policy race, price/cart change, COD/Mixed partial funding, double debit, settlement timeout, refunds, lost/late dispatch acceptance, publication rollback and media privacy. Verify persisted owner facts and explicitly report NOT_RUN where needed.

**Human-approved internal risk-assurance process (2026-11-13):** The Product owner adopts target policy without waiting for a nonexistent external reviewer. Before a real finance, authentication/security, protected migration/deletion or sensitive-data change is activated, perform attributable internal adversarial assessment with affected owner contracts, authorizations, negative cases, ledger/concurrency or restore evidence, and explicit human approval for the consequential action. If these proofs have not run, state `NOT_RUN`/`REVIEW_BLOCKED` for the *unverified implementation*, not `PASS`; a self-assertion, document, green pipeline or HTTP 200 does not verify it. No external audit or independent signature is represented as having occurred, and applicable legal consent/obligations remain binding.

## Database roles, enforceable constraints and bounded reads

Even where physical PostgreSQL infrastructure is shared, each service runtime identity may operate **only on its own schema/facts with the minimum needed privileges**; database schema administration and migration authority require distinct controlled credentials. A legitimate cross-owner read goes through a stated reviewed contract or bounded approved projection, not indiscriminate SQL access. Prove both permitted same-owner writes and denied unrelated-owner reads/writes.

Durable uniqueness, foreign-key relationships, `NOT NULL`, range/check constraints, transaction atomicity, concurrency idempotency and required authorization/scoping constraints must be enforced at the strongest practical persistence boundary whenever expressible. Client and service pre-validation are complementary, not replacements. Source text and domain records use verified UTF-8 without BOM; legacy external encodings are decoded only at ingestion boundaries and not guessed or double-decoded. Preserve Arabic Unicode, mixed-direction identifiers and original business facts through API/storage/display round trips when affected. A consumer-required byte-order mark is permitted for an explicitly justified export format (such as Excel-compatible CSV), not for normal source files.

Test or synthetic records are environment evidence, not Product truth: they may be disposed of only after proving test ownership, isolation, absence of shared/retained dependencies and cleanup authorization. Preserve unknown-origin and useful development records; do not add test-only columns or alternate writers merely to make tests easier.

Large collection reads must use owner-side bounded pagination/filter/sort, stable deterministic tie-breaking and permission-scoped cursors where necessary. A rejected/stale cursor must not leak another Store's data or silently return a different scope. Do not impose one universal paging method where the owner can justify a simpler correct approach.

## Failure contract, cross-owner degraded state and evidence

Material Owner APIs should expose stable **machine-classifiable error categories** and canonical operation/reconciliation identities. Human-readable Arabic messages are for users, not a parser for business decisions. Multi-record commands may complete partially only when their finite affected scope and per-item result are durably reconstructable; never present partial progress as an atomic batch success.

A missing second-owner acknowledgment in an authorization/money handoff leaves a clearly incomplete/degraded transaction; a successful local write or immutable historical snapshot cannot alone authorize an irreversible transfer. Unknown provider outcomes are reconciled before retries that might double move funds. Restart/resume reconstructs from current Owner facts rather than a stale in-memory replica or a client's cached success. A timeout changes a durable business state only when the owning capability defines that consequence; no silent fallback may switch canonical writer, authority or success meaning. Health/readiness distinguish configured, available, degraded and unknown states where material; tracing preserves correlation and cause without logging secrets or unnecessary personal data.

Material media has one lawful upload/reference/retention path; Store/Order attachments never become public via derived preview. Orphan cleanup requires an ownership/retention check, not blanket filesystem deletion. Retention, deletion and anonymization remain the fact owner's obligations and must not erase another owner's protected historical truth; media licensing, provenance and permitted visual use remain with the relevant Design/domain owner.
