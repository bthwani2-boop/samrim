## Common invariants

DSH owns cart, quote, checkout, Order, authoritative commercial price and adjustment for both sales channels. Identity verifies the actor; WLT alone authorizes funding and posts financial effects. One confirmed Store Order has one Store, immutable channel and chosen fulfillment mode, with original purchase evidence, payer/recipient, price/tax where applicable, operational/financial policy and agreement evidence. A public Direct page is neither payment authority nor a second Order engine. Protected checkout requires an authenticated purchasing Customer; any future guest checkout requires explicit Product, Identity, DSH and WLT admission and proof.

## SERVICEABILITY_ADDRESSES — Service cities and destination eligibility
CAPABILITY_ID: SERVICEABILITY_ADDRESSES

DSH owns the Location Core: canonical Store delivery origin coordinates and Customer-owned DeliveryAddress text and coordinates, their persistence, ownership and lawful execution readback. DSH alone owns Service City identity/lifecycle and the serviceability decision. Map/geocoder/router is a technical adapter. Each admitted Store and Address belongs to exactly one canonical city; Address ownership derives from the verified Customer session. Caller actor ID, location intent and browsing activeServiceCityId are not authority.

DSH evaluates canonical coordinates server-side for the current customer-visible Store, its actual city/origin, owned Address, active cities and effective versioned radius. Historical admitted CITY_SCOPE_V2_RADIUS_30KM is an inclusive 30,000-metre straight-line bound, not travel-time or road-distance guarantee:
- SERVICEABLE: valid same active city, eligible Store and within effective radius.
- UNSERVICEABLE: otherwise valid active known cities differ or distance exceeds radius.
- UNAVAILABLE: missing/legacy unassigned, inactive/unknown city, invalid coordinates, unpublished/ineligible Store or missing required canonical dependency/evidence.

Return Store/Address/City and policy-version freshness evidence without leaking coordinates across actors. Exact location is purpose-limited and minimized. Serviceability does not redefine publication or select another fulfillment lane. WLT quote independently rejects the same out-of-scope movement; price tiers cannot extend eligibility. Re-evaluate at quote/confirmation; no cached map, local preference or HTTP response alone proves success. DSH owns supported scoped radius policy; WLT consumes compatible effective version. Historical value is not a hardcoded client/SQL constant. Changes apply prospectively and cannot admit inactive cities or unknown coordinates.

## CART_CHECKOUT — Single-Store Cart and canonical confirmation
CAPABILITY_ID: CART_CHECKOUT

One logical cart per verified purchasing Customer and Store across entry surfaces. CartLine binds canonical StoreOffer, sellable Variant, exact quantity and selected modifier-option IDs, not free text or client totals. Every cart mutation has expected version and stable idempotency identity bound to its payload; stale version or changed payload conflicts. Explicit DIRECT/MARKETPLACE sales channel must satisfy [Store law](stores.md) at quote and confirmation; missing/unknown/prohibited channels fail closed. Historical Orders without verified channel evidence are not relabeled.

Issue a bounded short-lived owner quote; immediately before confirmation revalidate Store publication/ownership, availability, selected admitted mode, Offer/Variant/quantity/modifiers, price/promotion, stock, serviceability, consent and financial policy/agreement/payment eligibility. Fulfillment selection is independent from payment method; no silent fulfillment substitution. Client totals are advisory. Establish stable logical checkout/Order operation identity with immutable payload binding before any WLT effect; changed channel/cart/recipient/mode under that identity conflicts. WLT authorizes actual allocation, holds and subsequent effects; external funding usable only after verified internal posting. Customer allocation and beneficiary settlement are separate.

Recipient purpose SELF or OTHER; for OTHER freeze only name, reachable phone and instructions necessary for lawful execution. Purchaser remains Order/payment principal; recipient gains no Identity, wallet, refund or cancellation authority. Destination/recipient PII is revealed only to the permitted participant at the lawful stage, never prospect Captains or unrelated Stores. No unnecessary delivery address for pickup.

Confirm once with canonical snapshot, including applicable address/serviceability and Store/mode orderability evidence, and durable DSH/WLT readback. Customer and authorized Merchant consume the same Order identity. Stale cart, price/stock, refusal, offline or unknown cross-owner outcome remains truthful and reconciles by original identity before success or retrying a financial effect. Failed technical checkout rollback is not commercial cancellation.

## MULTI_STORE_CHECKOUT — Orchestration without merged Store truth
CAPABILITY_ID: MULTI_STORE_CHECKOUT

DSH parent checkout represents composition, child relation and status; no second legal Order/settlement writer. Every independent child retains Store, quote, inventory, independently eligible fulfillment, authority, financial effect identity, custody, exception/cancellation/refund and settlement liability. Combined client total never overrides child quotes. Parent cannot report COMPLETED while any required child/effect is pending or unknown. Explicitly expose partial results, compensate or cancel each child lawfully and reconcile original identities before retry. Grouped pickups/deliveries, when admitted by actual volume, retain individual custody/liability; no speculative route optimization.

## ORDER_LIFECYCLE — Durable state and lawful exceptions
CAPABILITY_ID: ORDER_LIFECYCLE

DSH owns acceptance, rejection, preparation, readiness, dispatch/pickup, finalization, cancellation, incident/refund requests and adjustments with current actor/Store authority, applicable readiness, expected Order version and concurrency-safe operation identity. Merchant acts only on owned/granted Store Orders. The immutable confirmation snapshot retains canonical Offer/Variant identity, purchased display name, variant/modifier evidence, requested quantity and unit/measure, pricing/amount, serviceability, selected mode, payer/recipient and applicable agreement/policy/consent evidence. Later fulfillment records a separate final fulfilled snapshot and append-only attributable adjustment history.

Actual quantity/substitution/removal requires lawful DSH adjustment against the canonical OrderLine and admitted catalog quantity semantics. Substitutes reference eligible canonical item/Variant; free text cannot replace transaction identity. Explicit Customer decision is required when the change materially alters what the Customer receives or pays; Merchant cannot approve on Customer's behalf. Unresolved required consent blocks affected readiness/terminal transition until lawful decision or exception/cancellation. WLT alone determines and applies/reconciles any financial delta; DSH never infers ledger completion. Retrying the same logical adjustment cannot duplicate adjustment or economic effect.

One explicitly chosen BTHWANI_CAPTAIN, PARTNER_CAPTAIN or CUSTOMER_PICKUP is frozen; no silent mode substitution or historical backfill without proof. Common Store preparation leads to mode-specific fulfillment readiness. DELIVERED, PICKED_UP, REJECTED and CANCELLED remain distinct terminal facts, not generic completed/payment status. Post-delivery/pickup wrong, damaged or missing items and other disputes may open bounded incident/refund review without rewriting the completed Order into a new sale, cash collection or successful refund. Generic returns/exchanges/warranty management is not admitted. Basic rating/feedback may attach to a completed Order independently of conversation closure; neither changes financial or custody truth. See [fulfillment](fulfillment.md).

### Cancellation and compensation ownership

DSH snapshots versioned Store Type cancellation policy at creation (OPERATIONS_ONLY or CLIENT_BEFORE_ACCEPTANCE). Customer can cancel only while the persisted rule and actual stage permit, before acceptance/dispatch when applicable. Authorized Operations handles bounded exceptions with reason/attribution; each multi-Store child has its own decision. WLT alone determines payment release, collected liability and exact refund/compensation entitlement. Pre-collection hold release is not extra credit; after collection unverified amount stays pending. No separate routine refund ticket is required. Apply [financial refund law](finance.md#refunds-and-compensation) for the narrow admitted platform-funded compensation and every actual posting; DSH closes only after required readback. Rare external exceptions belong to authorized Operations/Finance. Unknown funding/collection or ambiguous refund remains explicit reconciliation.

### Order-scoped contact and operational exceptions

Early-Customer telephone verification is a DSH Order-scoped Operations process, not a global trust authority, CRM, onboarding role, mandatory preparation prerequisite or payment override. A current operations-authorized actor acquires one exclusive expiring versioned contact claim per eligible Order before recording any outcome; renew/release/reclaim are authorized and attributable and do not change Order lifecycle. The first claim persists PENDING with zero attempts and no last-attempt timestamp. Only an actual call outcome advances the attempt count; a legacy zero-attempt placeholder does not establish a call. Record actor, time, expected version and idempotency without call audio or unnecessary copied profile data. Count distinct Orders only with actual spoken CONFIRMED contact and independently verified completed delivery/pickup; pending/no-answer/denied/invalid/abandoned never count. Do not retrospectively recall Customers with sufficiently proven prior completions or infer a public negative trust label. Qualification waives only these routine calls, never payment, custody, cancellation, Identity authorization or risk controls. Historical initial settings: first three Orders, ten-minute claim; DSH owns count, lease, attempt limit, timing and enabled scope. Changes never relabel an old contact. Blocking preparation pending contact needs explicit separate Product admission and actual DSH/WLT Wallet/COD/Mixed reservation and expiry proof.

DSH owns one bounded active exception per Order and reason, claim/owner, urgency, concrete next action, expected version and append-only attributable event. Manual resolution requires current operations permission, assigned case owner and explicit resolution evidence/note. Every operation serializes conflicting claims, decisions and idempotent replays. A later genuinely distinct occurrence may open after resolution; unchanged event or stage evidence may not reopen the resolved occurrence. Notes and cases cannot overwrite Order state, custody, cancellation, Customer status or WLT balance. Existing Operations views expose a bounded urgency-first active-case inbox, including unassigned and current-operator scopes, and an Order/case-scoped paginated audit timeline (actor, action, time, necessary detail); no internal correlation hashes, idempotency keys or irrelevant private facts. No generic helpdesk, CRM, route trail or finance workflow.

The existing authorized Order detail reads bounded, read-only canonical evidence references eligible for the exact Order/case; DSH revalidates the selected evidence and current authority in the resolution transaction. PAYMENT_EXCEPTION and DELIVERY_BLOCKED cannot close by note alone: require applicable order-bound verified WLT POSTED acknowledgment or verified delivery-completion audit and no unresolved relevant financial/custody obligations. Persist the canonical proof reference; deny stale, wrong-order, unverified or incomplete evidence. No simulated transfer/refund, and no fabricated closure during protected migration: historical closed cases without evidence retain their status/history and require explicit audit disposition under safety law.

One DSH detector derives bounded, audited unassigned attention from current authoritative stage/audit events. Historical noncontractual initial policy: CREATED or MERCHANT_ACCEPTED stagnation at 30 minutes; PREPARING beyond the Store preparation target (30-minute operational fallback); verified DELIVERY_FAILED; BTHWANI_CAPTAIN READY_FOR_DISPATCH without live offer/assignment after five minutes; at least two actual NO_ANSWER events on active nonconfirmed Orders; an unclaimed NORMAL case older than 30 minutes escalates once to URGENT/ACTION_REQUIRED while remaining unassigned. Exact executable names follow owner contracts; historical minute cadence and timings are configurable references, not hardcoded client constants or an SLA. Missing stage proof is UNKNOWN, not delay or Merchant fault. One miss, confirmed/terminal Orders, active offer, assigned delivery and claimed/resolved cases are excluded from the corresponding automatic attention/escalation. No detector may delay initial contact, cancel an Order, mark trust or change custody/money.

DSH stores immutable approved stage targets by fulfillment mode, policy version and effective time. Current authorized platform_policies and applicable domain scope govern new versions, expected version, attributable reason and idempotent readback. Pin target from the policy effective when the actual canonical stage started; absent approval/event is UNKNOWN. Historical operational fallback and urgency are not contractual SLA. No default contractual target from unreviewed historical values. Only genuine forward progress after opening may auto-resolve eligible unclaimed automatic early-stage attention, with one attributable event; claimed cases and unresolved financial/custody disputes never silently close. Concurrent scans converge. Further contractual escalation types require actual owner admission/proof.

Journeys FIRST_THREE_ORDER_CONTACT, ORDER_SCOPED_OPERATIONS_EXCEPTION and ORDER_ADJUSTMENT_EXCEPTION_REFUND own cross-surface/supporting proof, not a second writer.

## ORDER_CONVERSATION — Order-scoped communication
CAPABILITY_ID: ORDER_CONVERSATION

DSH owns one Store Order conversation, membership, message identity/content and attachment association, per-participant read state, moderation, lawful retention and scoped access. Membership derives from the current purchasing Customer, authorized Store workers, actively assigned Captain and appropriately scoped Operations. Sender chooses one or more operational recipients; DSH filters message reads/read receipts to sender, selected recipients and authorized Operations. Authorized Operations may review all messages of the scoped Order in its existing detail; composer is explicitly opt-in and requires current operations permission. No prospect Captain, unrelated Store, revoked actor, public registration or stale notification link grants private contact/media access.

Text is baseline. Historical initial per-message attachment choices: one image JPEG/PNG/WebP up to 3 MiB, one voice MP4/M4A/3GPP/AAC/WebM up to 1.5 MiB, or one operational video MP4/WebM up to 6 MiB. Validate actual bytes and effective owner-approved limits. Store attachment privately under DSH authority; every retrieval verifies same Order, current participant, per-message recipients or explicit Operations permission. No public URL, MIME spoof, upload or forwarded link bypass. Commit message and private attachment reference atomically; retries preserve original identity, media content and recipients, rejecting changed payload. Native recording/playback requiring a new module needs compatible Android/iOS build; text and photo chat remain available on supported older builds. Lawful deletion/redaction preserves required audit meaning.

Terminal DELIVERED, PICKED_UP, REJECTED or CANCELLED immediately rejects new sends while retaining authorized reads and committed replay without a fresh write; rating does not control closure. Offline send, duplicate retry, media failure, membership change and read/unread retry converge to one DSH truth. Notification delivery is a projection of owner events, not message authority; follow [architecture](architecture.md) for provider boundaries. Message, support escalation or media cannot mutate Order, payment, payout, refund, settlement or custody.

## COMMERCE_PROMOTIONS — One promotional price/eligibility truth
CAPABILITY_ID: COMMERCE_PROMOTIONS

Admitted forms: Offer/Product discounts, bounded category/group discounts, Store/Order threshold discounts, coupons and authorized platform campaigns. Merchant owner or current delegate may author only granted Stores, including selected/all authorized Stores through multi-Store UX with explicit per-Store scopes. Platform campaigns may require individual Merchant opt-in; display is not consent. Loyalty, points, membership, generic marketing automation, AI targeting, dynamic pricing and broad segmentation are not implied by this admission.

DSH owns eligibility, authoritative price and redemption; WLT owns economic funding. No duplicate price calculator. Draft, scheduled/active and ended/cancelled remain distinct. Server time, usage counters and replay are concurrency-safe; stacking forbidden absent explicit effective policy. Checkout revalidates eligibility server-side. Funding is PARTNER (merchant), BTHWANI or SHARED with exact split, owner-selected rather than client-selected. Each applicable Order freezes promotion identity/version, Store scope, discount amount and funding allocation separately from its base price. Unknown/expired/exhausted/refused outcomes fail closed and reconcile with WLT. Customer receives effective discount/coupon evidence, not private subsidy allocation, accounting or WLT internals. Cancellation/refund uses frozen transaction evidence and reconciles original funding once; no retrospective rewrite of base Offer price, confirmed Order, funding or earnings.

## DISCOVERY_CONTENT — Governed content-to-Store journeys
CAPABILITY_ID: DISCOVERY_CONTENT

One DSH content owner governs identity, placement, eligibility, draft, processing, moderation, approval, publication, attribution, expiry, scope, canonical readback and takedown of admitted banners, carousels, short-form and other approved content. Targets are canonical STORE, PRODUCT, CATEGORY or PROMOTION as appropriate, or explicitly admitted bounded informational targets. Merchant with current Store promotions grant may draft SHORT_FORM or image BANNER only for its own source_store_id and own STORE/PRODUCT/PROMOTION, not CATEGORY. Operator retains publication authority.

Homepage platform banners are Marketing Operator-controlled and exclude Merchant-sourced Store media; Store banners are that Store's approved in-window images, never unrelated ordinary products as empty-slot fallback. For a DIRECT Store the same DSH engine serves approved Store-owned image banners only on its legally published public Store page, with own reviewed Store/legal/policy facts and self-Store destination. No marketplace flag, homepage inclusion, short-form distribution or Merchant approval shortcut. Withdrawal of the public Store removes banner eligibility at read time. Merchant hide/restore of own approved banner is separately versioned display choice, not new publication or override of Operator pause.

Short-form uses bounded media limits in [catalog](catalog.md#owner-backed-limits), validated normalized mobile H.264/AAC and immutable generated JPEG poster. Failed normalization/target visibility never publishes; takedown/cleanup reconciles video/poster and retention. Provider is replaceable technical storage/delivery, not content identity. App-local hardcoded content is not publication authority. Autoplay/media follow accessibility, bandwidth and platform constraints in [experience](experience.md). No social platform or alternate publication/checkout authority. Stale/removed links and missing media degrade safely; category labels, previews and videos never grant eligibility. Monetary promotion effects remain with COMMERCE_PROMOTIONS and WLT.

## Commercial measurement and campaign effectiveness

Distinguish successful zero search results, query failure, unknown serviceability, unavailable catalog and missing observation. Keep city/channel/filter scope and eligible catalog basis. Interest, views, clicks, favorites and cart changes are not fulfilled demand. Funnel metrics specify cohort/window, distinct units, denominators and missing observations. Confirmation, acceptance, preparation, assignment, delivery/pickup and cancellation derive from respective owner facts; do not double-count parent/children, retries or failures.

Campaign metrics distinguish eligibility, exposure, interaction, redemption, confirmation, fulfillment and reversal. Attribution is not causal uplift. Before increasing exposure verify WLT funding/budget/usage, current Store/Offer eligibility and actual capacity. Recommendations cannot activate discounts or reserve money. Actionable anomalies use existing exceptions/readiness, with reason, source, scope, next action and owner-backed resolution; reopen invalidated evidence. Apply [measurement law](architecture.md#measurement-integrity-and-intelligence-admission) and [financial reporting](finance.md#order-economics-and-financial-measurement).
