## Common invariants
DSH is the **one** owner of cart, quote, checkout, Order, price and adjustment truth for Marketplace and Direct. Each confirmed Store Order has one Store ID, frozen authoritative price/units/tax-if-applicable/policy/agreement/fulfillment/payment allocation snapshot. WLT handles payment authorization, holds, ledger and settlement; Identity handles actor/authorization. Direct Web checkout (guest or authenticated as explicitly approved) reuses DSH Order engine and may not spawn a second direct-orders table or cart state machine.

## SERVICEABILITY_ADDRESSES — Service cities and destination eligibility
CAPABILITY_ID: SERVICEABILITY_ADDRESSES

DSH owns canonical service-city approval, location coordinates/address accuracy, address scope, distance/effective serviceability and route eligibility. Address/recipient PII is minimized, scoped and never exposed to candidate Captains before lawful assignment. The donor CITY_SCOPE_V2_RADIUS_30KM semantics require compatibility review against actual owner policy before encoding numbers in new SQL; no hardcoded UI city bypass. A geolocation fix or client map position is not proof of serviceable Order delivery. Re-evaluate location/selected fulfillment at confirmation with versioned owner decisions.

### Canonical serviceability outcome and evidence retained from donor 41

For each requested Customer-owned `DeliveryAddress` and target Customer-visible Store, **DSH** evaluates the actual stored `Store.service_city_id`, `DeliveryAddress.service_city_id`, current canonical active Service City, stored Store-origin/Address coordinates and effective, versioned city-radius policy. An Address belongs to the verified purchasing Customer, **not** to a caller-supplied actor ID; discovery `activeServiceCityId` is a browsing preference, never an eligibility decision. A provider/geocoder result is an input, not an alternative DSH authority. Each Store and Address has exactly one canonical Service City assignment when admitted.

DSH distinguishes three outcomes rather than collapsing a failed dependency into a false out-of-range result: `SERVICEABLE` only when the Store is customer-visible and the owned Address meets the same active-city and inclusive operating-radius conditions; `UNSERVICEABLE` for otherwise valid, active, known cities whose identities differ or whose authoritative distance exceeds the radius; and `UNAVAILABLE` for missing/legacy unassigned/inactive/unknown city, invalid coordinates, unpublished Store or missing canonical evidence/dependency. The DSH response carries **Store/Address/City and policy version evidence** for checkout freshness checks, without leaking precise coordinates to other actors. The original `CITY_SCOPE_V2_RADIUS_30KM` is initial versioned policy evidence (30,000-metre straight-line inclusive bound), not a new code constant or permission to extend WLT delivery quotes. Stale state is re-evaluated from the Owner; no success is asserted from a cached map or HTTP transport response.

## CART_CHECKOUT — Single-Store Cart and canonical confirmation
CAPABILITY_ID: CART_CHECKOUT

A user can compose a Store cart, receive one short-lived server quote, and confirm only if current Store publication, owner, price, stock, quantity, consent, serviceability, selected fulfillment and payment all remain eligible. UI totals are advisory; DSH authoritatively prices from StoreOffer and WLT authorizes funds. Confirmations have bounded idempotency, unknown-result reconciliation and no duplicate Orders or charges. Cancellation/refund eligibility is a versioned policy and original Order fact; legal changes post confirmation use DSH adjustments and WLT effects, never overwrite the initial snapshot.

### Authenticated CartLine, recipient and once-only confirmation

A logical Cart is scoped to **one verified purchasing Customer and one Store**, not merely to a device token or arbitrary request Store ID. Each CartLine binds one canonical `StoreOffer` and `ProductVariant`, exact requested quantity and selected modifier-option IDs; a Client cannot substitute free-text product identities or computed totals. DSH checks the current Store's publication, `STORE_OPERATIONAL_AVAILABILITY`, exact item/variant/modifier and quantity validity, actual stock/price, Service City eligibility and the **specifically selected available fulfillment mode** immediately before confirmation. Fulfillment is independent of the payment method; DSH must reject an unavailable selected mode without silently choosing another. Checkout rereads owner versions even when a prior quote, promotion or browser result appeared valid.

The delivery-recipient purpose is explicitly `SELF` or `OTHER`. For `OTHER`, DSH may freeze only the name, reachable phone and delivery instructions required for lawful execution; the **purchasing Customer stays the authenticated Order/payment principal**, and recipient details cannot create a second Identity Actor, permission, Wallet or claim to refund/order authority. Private destination/recipient data is revealed only at the permitted fulfillment stage and with current Order-scoped authorization; unrelated Stores and prospect Captains never inherit it.

Before invoking any WLT payment/collection effect, DSH establishes **one stable logical checkout/Order operation identity** with immutable payload binding. A repeated key with a different cart, recipient or mode is a conflict, never a second Order or charge. Original confirmation readback freezes canonical offer/variant/quantity/modifier, recipient/address, Store/city, selected fulfillment, effective price/promotion and policy/agreement version evidence. WLT alone owns customer payment allocation through `ORDER_PAYMENT_COLLECTION`, and external top-ups are usable only after `CUSTOMER_BALANCE_FUNDING` has posted a verified canonical internal balance; future merchant settlement allocation is a different WLT obligation. If cross-owner state is unknown, reconcile by the same identity and committed owner readback before reporting success or retrying an effect.

## MULTI_STORE_CHECKOUT — Orchestration without merged Store truth
CAPABILITY_ID: MULTI_STORE_CHECKOUT

A multi-Store shopping intent may coordinate multiple independent Store Orders under one orchestrated customer experience. Each Store retains its own Store Order, pricing, inventory, fulfillment, merchant permission and settlement liability. Failure in one child has explicit cancellation/compensation/partial-result semantics; a parent does not become a separate legal Order writer or conceal mixed financial results. No client-provided combined total overrides Store-level quotes.

### Parent/child completion and grouped-operation limit

The DSH parent checkout container represents **composition and status readback**, never a second legal Order or settlement writer. Each accepted child holds its own Store, fulfillment mode, WLT effect identity, cancellation/adjustment/refund state and exception evidence. The parent cannot report `COMPLETED` while any child or its financial effect is pending/unknown; retries of a partial child create must reconcile each original child identity before another attempt. Grouped pickups/deliveries may coordinate lawful children while preserving individual custody and per-Store liability, but this capability does **not** admit a speculative advanced route-optimization engine.

## ORDER_LIFECYCLE — Durable state and lawful exceptions
CAPABILITY_ID: ORDER_LIFECYCLE

DSH owns Order states, Store acceptance, preparation, dispatch/pickup, finalization, cancellation, adjustments and bounded incident/refund requests. State transition validates actor and Store relationship, exact Order version, publication/fulfillment eligibility and concurrency; a retry does not repeat side effects. Actual fulfilled quantity, substitution or removal after confirmation is an attributable adjustment + WLT delta, not a silent mutation of the legal Order. Operations intervention must be bounded and auditable, not a separate Order workflow.

## ORDER_CONVERSATION — Order-scoped communication
CAPABILITY_ID: ORDER_CONVERSATION

Participants may communicate via admitted messages and private video/audio evidence only within the Order-scoped, role-appropriate access window. DSH owns message/attachment association, moderation/legal retention and read rights; media transport/storage is technical. A Captain not yet assigned, unrelated Store or revoked actor must not receive customer phone/address or private media. Stale notification links cannot create unauthorized access.

**Message audience and terminal semantics:** A message belongs to one Store Order. DSH derives membership from the current purchasing Customer, authorized Store workers, the **currently assigned** Captain and correctly scoped Operations. Sender-selected recipient sets constrain visibility and read receipts to the sender, designated recipients and authorized Operations, not every participant who once touched that Order. Operations may review an Order's permitted messages but can compose only with an explicit current `operations` permission. Message send and its private attachment reference commit atomically under the **same idempotency identity**. A verified terminal `DELIVERED`, `PICKED_UP`, `REJECTED` or `CANCELLED` Order rejects fresh messages while keeping previously committed messages available to currently authorized viewers. Replaying a committed send is readback, not a new message; Order feedback cannot reopen chat.

**Private attachments:** The source-approved initial attachment set includes one image (JPEG/PNG/WebP, up to 3 MiB), one voice clip (MP4/M4A/3GPP/AAC/WebM, up to 1.5 MiB) or one operational video (MP4/WebM, up to 6 MiB) per message, validated against actual bytes and owner-approved effective media limits. These are candidate initial bounded settings, **not** permissions to expose public URLs or download another recipient's file. An upload, forwarded notification, malformed MIME type or stale read link cannot bypass Order/recipient authorization or turn chat into Order, custody or payment mutation. Media retention and native recording module availability follow the existing owner/platform gates; text remains viable without a newly rebuilt media module.

## COMMERCE_PROMOTIONS — One promotional price/eligibility truth
CAPABILITY_ID: COMMERCE_PROMOTIONS

Approved campaigns, discounts and sponsored discovery may affect presentation and customer price only through their canonical DSH eligibility and WLT funding/settlement contracts. A price promotion has scope, dates, funding source, usage limits and an auditable snapshot; stacking conflicts and races fail safely. Do not invent a second Offer/Order price calculator in promotions, Operator UI, video feed or Direct Store Web. Unknown funding does not silently become platform liability.

**Campaign admission and private funding evidence:** A Partner Owner or current Store-scoped delegate may author only promotions for Stores granted through `STORE_SCOPED_ACCESS_DELEGATION`, including a deliberate multi-Store authoring convenience that still persists explicit **per-Store** scopes. A platform campaign may require each participating Partner's opt-in; merely showing a campaign cannot record consent. Durable campaign meaning separates draft, scheduled/active and ended/cancelled conditions; eligibility, server time windows and redemption counters are concurrency-safe and idempotent. The Customer receives only the effective offer/discount label and coupon result needed to decide, **not** the Partner's subsidy allocation, accounting entries or WLT internals. A coupon or campaign cannot retrospectively rewrite a confirmed Order, base StoreOffer price or the original frozen `PARTNER`/`BTHWANI`/`SHARED` funding split; unknown/expired/exhausted/funding-refused outcomes fail closed and reconcile with WLT before success.

## DISCOVERY_CONTENT — Governed content-to-Store journeys
CAPABILITY_ID: DISCOVERY_CONTENT

Merchant/operator banners, short-form media, categories and applicable discovery surfaces route customers to valid Store/Offer targets only after approval, relevance and visibility checks. Media creation, upload, processing, safe publication, moderation, takedown and attribution are bounded DSH/content facts. Videos are not a generic social platform. A stale or removed promotional link must degrade safely without bypassing Store ownership or stock/checkout gating.

**Placement, approval and no-content fallback:** An authenticated Merchant with the current Store-scoped `promotions` grant may **submit a draft** `SHORT_FORM` or image `BANNER` only for its own authorized `source_store_id` and a `STORE`, `PRODUCT` or `PROMOTION` destination belonging to that Store; `CATEGORY` is not an admitted Merchant-owned destination. The Operator retains actual publication authority. The platform homepage banner is Marketing Operator-controlled; Merchant Store banners show only that Store's approved, published, in-window images and **must not silently fill empty banner slots with unrelated ordinary products**. Independent-Store Web banners are separately eligible only on the Store's lawful public page, not a backdoor into marketplace/homepage/short-form publication. Merchant hide/restore is a display choice on an already approved Store banner, never authorization to publish a draft or override an Operator pause. Failed media normalization/target visibility causes no publication; content does not authorize a bypass to checkout.

## Effective serviceability policy inherited for continuity

Branch-41 approved donor semantics include `CITY_SCOPE_V2_RADIUS_30KM`: Store and **owned** Customer DeliveryAddress must resolve to the **same active canonical Service City**, and their authoritative coordinates must be within the **current versioned DSH service-radius policy** (historical initial setting: a **30,000-metre inclusive straight-line radius**). Bad/unknown coordinates, inactive/unassigned city or absent owner readback fail closed. WLT's admitted delivery quote must reject the same out-of-scope movement; a longer-distance fee tier is not an authorization to bypass the delivery boundary.

Treat 30 km as the reviewed **initial effective business policy**, not an immutable number scattered throughout client or SQL. Changing a supported radius later occurs via a versioned, owner-controlled scoped policy in Operator Policies with DSH and WLT compatible readback and explicit operational/legal admissibility. Client city filters are presentation preferences only; they cannot influence the server's canonical Store/Address city determination. Straight-line reachability is not a road travel-time guarantee. Confirm the effective policy and price **again** at Order acceptance so an old map preview or alternate app route cannot bypass it.

## Cancellation and compensation ownership

One DSH-owned versioned cancellation policy applies to the relevant Store Type and is snapped onto the Order at creation. The permitted options include `OPERATIONS_ONLY` and `CLIENT_BEFORE_ACCEPTANCE`. Customer-initiated cancellation is permitted only while the persisted Order policy and actual Store/dispatch stage allow it; an authorized Operator can handle bounded exceptions with reason and attribution. Each child Store Order in a multi-Store checkout receives its own cancellation decision.

Pre-collection release of a WLT hold is **not** an extra credit. For already collected payments, compensation/refund requires evidence of entitlement, a balanced WLT posting, correct funding source and idempotent replay. The narrow donor-approved platform-funded compensation for a confirmed collected, customer-approved full-item removal from a completed Order, with **no promotion or discount**, must be separately checked and capped to the original line amount. All other ambiguous refund situations remain open for authorized WLT/DSH reconciliation rather than guessing a customer credit or silently reducing Store earnings.

## Promotion funding and discovery-media safety

The presently admitted promotion forms are StoreOffer/Product discounts, bounded category/product-group discounts, Store/Order threshold discounts, coupon codes, and authorized platform campaigns that may require individual Merchant opt-in. Funding is explicitly `PARTNER` (merchant), `BTHWANI` (platform) or `SHARED` with exact split; DSH owns eligibility/redemption and WLT owns **who economically funds** the discount. Stacking is forbidden unless an explicit effective policy allows it. Redemption has time/use limits, conflict/replay safety and an immutable Order-side funding/version snapshot; an edited campaign never retroactively rewrites base StoreOffer prices or earnings.

Discovery content targets valid DSH Store, Product, Category or Promotion references only as authorized for its author. A Merchant-authored short video or image banner is limited to its **own Store / Product / Promotion**, not an arbitrary global Category; the Operator retains publication approval. Homepage platform banners and Merchant Store banners have distinct authorization/placement policies but a **single DSH content publication owner**. A DIRECT Store banner may appear on its legal public Store page without entering marketplace discovery or copying the homepage writer. Merchant hide/restore of an already approved Store banner does not grant the right to publish an unapproved draft.

The currently bounded short-form source requires at most **10 MiB and 30 seconds**, valid normalized mobile-oriented H.264/AAC media and a generated immutable JPEG poster. Failed processing must not publish the content, and approved takedown/cleanup reconciles video and poster references safely under retention. These values are owner-controlled media policy, not extra client upload authority. Category labels or media previews never become replacement Store/Offer/Order eligibility engines.

## Operator-managed commercial thresholds — explicit policy, not compiled law

The historical **30 km** service radius is the initial effective DSH value for an eligible active Service City; it is not a constant in SQL/client/code. An authorized Operator can set a supported radius per admitted city or relation where actually needed. DSH owns eligibility at checkout/Order confirmation; WLT pricing/quote must use the same effective validated policy version. A changed radius cannot activate an unapproved city, allow unknown coordinates or retroactively modify accepted Orders.

DSH owns configurable **delivery distance bands, fees, additional distance charges, active Order limits, preparation/ETA escalation thresholds, cancellation windows, quantities, promotion validity/redemption and compensation eligibility/ceilings** according to the individual proper owner and verified capabilities. Amounts actually owed, refunded, settled or billed remain WLT-owned; a DSH pricing policy may produce a quoted charge but cannot post a WLT ledger credit itself.

Do not interpret the historic 30 km, a cancellation option, delivery fee, short-form media limit or promotion threshold as a **hardcoded immutable Product law**. The Operator Policies UX may change approved values with validation, bounded scope, version, effective date, preview and durable readback. An implementation must not silently accept a change to the underlying data without actual service/application support. Changing a supported rule requires no code deployment; adding a *new kind of operation* still requires implementation and security/financial admission. Unapproved old Orders retain their frozen commercial terms.

## DSH Order-scoped customer contact and Operations exceptions

The **initially admitted** early-Customer telephone verification is a **DSH Order-scoped Operations process**, not a global Customer-trust authority, another onboarding role, a separate CRM, a prerequisite for Merchant preparation or permission to interrupt COD/Wallet/Mixed. DSH records factual contact attempts with current Operations permission, short versioned claim ownership, expiry and idempotency. Actual spoken `CONFIRMED` contact and **independently verified completed delivery/pickup** are both required before a distinct Order counts toward the early-Order contact objective; `PENDING`, `NO_ANSWER`, denial, invalid number and abandoned claim never count as confirmation. Customers with sufficiently proven historical completions are not recalled retrospectively, and no public negative trust label is inferred.

The count of first Orders (historic initial value **three**), claim lease (historic initial **ten minutes**), follow-up attempt limit, enabled scope and attention timing are **versioned DSH Operator Policies**, not client/source constants. A change must not recalculate or relabel an old contact as an actual call. The separate ability to block preparation pending contact requires explicit Product admission and genuine DSH/WLT payment-state proofs before any toggle can enable it.

DSH also owns **one bounded Order-scoped operational exception** record per active Order and reason, with current case owner, urgency, concrete next action and append-only attributable event. The existing Order detail/Operations workspace reads this case via permission-scoped, server-paginated queries. An `OPERATIONS` note cannot overwrite Order state or WLT balances. `PAYMENT_EXCEPTION` and `DELIVERY_BLOCKED` require a verified specific posted financial acknowledgment, valid delivery proof or canonical resolution of outstanding obligations—not an arbitrary close button.

Owner-confirmed early-stage stagnation, unavailable dispatch, delivery failure or repeated actual no-answer attempts may create **deduplicated attention** cases under named DSH Operator Policies (historic non-contractual examples: ~30-minute early-stage review, ~5-minute no-offer review, two actual no-answer events, ~30-minute unclaimed escalation). No stage event/approved contractual SLA means **UNKNOWN**, never an invented breach. An approved stage target is versioned and pinned at actual stage start; current changed policy cannot retroactively alter an earlier deadline. Actual forward transitions can auto-close only eligible **unclaimed** automated early-stage attention; a claimed case or unresolved custody/financial dispute is never silently closed. Repeated detectors/concurrent workers must converge on one case/event with clear idempotency and bounded readback.

The specific cross-surface evidence and failure/recovery paths are owned by the `FIRST_THREE_ORDER_CONTACT`, `ORDER_SCOPED_OPERATIONS_EXCEPTION` and `ORDER_ADJUSTMENT_EXCEPTION_REFUND` journeys in `journeys.md`; this is **not** an additional Order writer or a second policy registry.

## Durable mode and Order terminal-state semantics

Every canonical DSH Store Order freezes exactly **one explicitly chosen fulfillment mode** from the admitted set: `BTHWANI_CAPTAIN`, `PARTNER_CAPTAIN`, or `CUSTOMER_PICKUP`. A chosen mode is not a payment method, Store relation, marketing channel or default that the server may silently substitute when the original mode becomes unavailable. Historical mode introduction requires explicit and proven backfill semantics, never relabeling old Orders merely to fit a new enum.

The legally distinct `DELIVERED`, `PICKED_UP`, `REJECTED` and `CANCELLED` outcomes cannot be collapsed into a generic UI `completed` label as a payment or entitlement decision. DSH maintains one Order confirmation snapshot (Store ID, Variant/Offer ID, quantities, modifiers, fulfillment, agreed financial/operational policy versions, payer/recipient and monetary price basis) separate from a later fulfilled snapshot and append-only adjustment audit. An unresolved **customer-required** substitution, quantity change or refund agreement blocks the affected readiness/terminal transition; Merchant cannot silently approve on the Customer's behalf. WLT separately evaluates actual payable amount, held cash and legally required refund/settlement delta with once-only postings.

Failed fulfillment or dispute may create a bounded exception without mutating a historical terminal Order to look like a new sale, physical COD collection or successful refund. Any draft new mode or cancellation setting must first be admitted into real DSH/WLT contracts and owner-backed Operator Policies; security, custody proof and ledger conservation are not configurable switches.
