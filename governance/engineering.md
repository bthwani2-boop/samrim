### Mandatory removal and clean refoundation

**Human-approved 2026-11-26 — NON-NEGOTIABLE.** Every authorized remediation, even one defect, requires immediate evaluation and clean reconstruction of the complete affected foundation. The unit of execution is the whole necessary defective file, unit and foundation, not the reported line, function, symptom, warning, test or finding. This law applies to source, architecture, contracts, data, configuration, governance, instructions, skills, tools, tests, synchronization, infrastructure, Docker, tunnels and CI/CD.

1. Before any corrective edit, discover the complete affected responsibility and its authored files, units, settings, contracts, dependencies, direct and dynamic consumers, outputs, tests, retained obligations, data and applicable runtime. Establish the cause and classify the whole affected foundation from actual evidence; no find-fix-next-finding loop. A related failure or new finding reopens that disposition.
2. **DELETE:** permanently remove 100% of proven unnecessary, redundant, obsolete, conflicting-without-necessary-responsibility, superseded and workaround components from authorized active scope, including unnecessary references, settings and derived effects. No moving, renaming, disabling, stubbing, hiding or recreating the losing implementation. Deletion precedes replacement when safe; otherwise transfer behavior, consumers, lawful rights and retained state first, prove the transition, then remove the losing implementation. A necessary bounded transition has an actual owner, removal condition, evidence and outstanding acceptance; it is never final closure.
3. **REFOUND:** fully reconstruct 100% of necessary defective files, units and foundations from canonical requirements, retaining required sound behavior and obligations, not the losing design. Eliminate the root cause and class of failure, reestablish affected contracts, dependencies and consumers, and regenerate derived artifacts from corrected owners. Same-path reconstruction is valid; a physical deletion ritual, diff size or file count proves nothing. A new component requires proved distinct necessity.
4. **KEEP:** retain only positively evidenced necessary sound components. Do not rewrite sound source merely for symmetry, a quota or to demonstrate severity; necessary consumer integration is allowed without concealing a defective implementation. Unknown is not proof of soundness or permission to delete. Preserve upstream-managed original tool assets and update through verified compatible official releases under authorization.
5. No patching, individual error pursuit, line/function repair, temporary fallback as final solution, weakened tests, self-granted exception, deferred cleanup or duplicated mechanisms. Neither small scope, urgency, cost, successful tests, workload, parallel work, a transport problem nor historical acceptance exempts a defect. Only a separately explicit human instruction naming an intentionally narrow correction outside active remediation can authorize that different method; it cannot exempt an outstanding refoundation or override protection. A general request to fix or refound is never such an exception.
6. Protect data, credentials, history, signing, external identities, lawful records, financial rights and other writers. Separate protected-effect authorization remains binding; never bypass DENIED. Resolve technical impediments by authorized alternatives, reconcile unknown effects before retry, identify actual blocking resource, evidence, dependency, owner and next action; continue independent authorized work.
7. Before acceptance, reconcile the original and discovered scope against the final source, contracts, Git and runtime evidence. Prove DELETE absence and reference removal, whole-file REFOUND, necessary sound KEEP and necessity of new files. Prove required behavior, consumer transfer, one owner/writer/engine, negative authorization, durable effects, concurrency, recovery, provenance and recurrence prevention as applicable. Strengthen the existing owner guard for a mechanically testable gap; prove positive and rejected cases without suppression or a parallel checker. No unclassified file, known defect, obsolete reference or missing mandatory proof in an accepted scope. Deliver complete tested owned batches immediately; keep the remaining scope open.
