## What this directory means

This is BThwani's single active durable semantic governance root. The current approved domain decisions direct progressive capability development and acceptance; the index does not own their detailed rules, engineering procedures or implementation state. One decision has one semantic owner. Resolve proven contradictions at the appropriate owner under actual authority.

Historic docs/governance/ and the read-only archive/legacy-pre-refoundation-20261114, ZIP archives, dated reports and original research are donor evidence, not another editable policy root. Preserve materially binding historical legal/financial records and source provenance through the existing [documentation index](../docs/README.md) and relevant owner; absence of a migrated clause is not approval to retire it.

## Authority and conflict protocol

A latest explicit human approval for the same subject supersedes incompatible earlier choices, preserving compatible obligations. Determine values, permissions and legal status from the live owner and actual evidence, never from archived implementation alone. Reconcile historical rights and obligations before changing or retiring them. Unresolved material choices fail closed for their affected action; no unauthorized activation or guessed default. Only the owner may record a substantive decision; cross-owner consumers link to it.

Implementation state requires actual source, executable contracts, relevant data/schema and runtime owner readback. Documents, scaffolds, mocks and policy approval do not certify live service behavior, financial safety or legal compliance.

Protected operations, destructive effects, credentials, Git and external activation retain their action-specific boundaries.

## What must be true before governance cutover

GOVERNANCE_CUTOVER: NOT_READY records unfinished whole-platform historical semantic reconciliation only. It does not block progressive development, independent accepted increments or a fully proven journey, and is not production readiness.

Only this index may record GOVERNANCE_CUTOVER: READY after all of these are evidenced and the reviewed policy revision is approved:

- All 14 canonical domain documents are substantively reconciled against accepted decisions, relevant original source/migrations and material archived obligations. All admitted Product IDs, including the original 26, route exactly once to their substantive owners; no material subfeature, right or obligation is silently lost.
- The owner boundaries and cross-references agree, with no active legacy governance, duplicate writer/engine, unresolved critical business conflict or generic unbounded policy executor. Operator-editable rules belong to their actual owners.
- The sole human Product owner has approved target commercial, architectural, security, migration and legal policy choices under the 2026-11-13 decision. No external consultant or independent sign-off is required merely to settle those choices. Record actual normative financial rules, threat model, payout/ledger formulas and protected migration strategy with attributable approval and internal adversarial risk assessment.
- The applicable policy-level consistency and reconciliation evidence identifies the exact reviewed revision and approval. No retired guard, duplicate approval dossier or separate certification claim. Unresolved material risks block only affected actions.

## Cutover readiness is a policy decision, not a claim of implemented software

READY certifies that the approved target Product/design/API/SQL/operational policy is consistently reconciled and can direct scoped implementation. It does not certify built applications, API parity, three implemented SQL baselines, checkout E2E, production release or real money/legal effects.

Unsafe or unverified technical implementation remains unactivated until its internal critical-risk assessment, applicable negative/adversarial tests, protected-record reconciliation, actual runtime proof and applicable real-world legal/contract evidence are sufficient. Apply the engineering and safety owners to each affected action. Missing evidence is NOT_RUN/REVIEW_BLOCKED/FAIL as appropriate, not PASS. Never fabricate independent review or a historical signature; policy approval, authority to edit and historical Push are not authorization to merge, ship or deploy.
